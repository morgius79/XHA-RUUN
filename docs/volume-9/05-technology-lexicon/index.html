The technical lexicon of Xha'Ruun is wholly autochthonous: not a single term is borrowed from the languages of the Kel'vash or the Moryn (→ see [Ch.09], [Ch.10] — borrowings are confined to biology and culture). New concepts are formed by composing existing roots, which makes each term self-explaining: tharkhal (reactor) reads to a native speaker as "energy-life" without recourse to a dictionary.
