The defining feature of the social lexicon: concepts of power are built around the root rass (unity), not the root of force thar. Coercion is expressed through tor ("away, from") — that is, always as separation from the collective rather than as the application of force. This is linguistic evidence of the collegial order (→ see [Volume III, Ch.01]).
