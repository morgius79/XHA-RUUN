"Théxar gave us everything — metal, energy, water, land. Our duty is to take only as much as we can return." — The Codex of the Keepers, +1,200 Y.E.

Introduction

The elevated metallicity of the protoplanetary cloud of Khar'Vex ([Fe/H] = +0.12) gave Théxar a unique mineral base. This chapter maps the distribution of key resources, described in detail in → see [Volume I, Ch.19].
