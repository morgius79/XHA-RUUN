Volume VIII: Atlas — Chapter 11: Map of the Star System

"Seven planets — seven notes of one symphony. Each on its own orbit, each in its own rhythm. Théxar is the melody of life sounding in this stellar orchestra." — From "The Harmony of the Spheres," an astronomical treatise, +3,200 Y.E.

Introduction

The Khar'Vex star system is the home of the Xha'Ruun civilization. The orange K2V dwarf, seven planets, two asteroid belts, and an extensive space infrastructure form a single economic complex. This chapter systematizes the astronomical data, described in detail in → see [Volume I, Ch.32].
