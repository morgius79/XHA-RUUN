Volume VIII: Atlas — Chapter 7: Climate Zones

"Climate is the character of a land. The equator is its passion, the poles its wisdom. And between them — the infinite diversity of life." — From "The Geography of Théxar," the Academy of Khar-Vexar

Related sections: → see [Volume I, Ch.15, "Atmosphere and Climate"], → see [Volume I, Ch.37, "Ecological Zones"], → see [Volume VIII, Ch.01, "Planetary Map"], → see [Volume I, Ch.13, "Continents"] Length: 15 pages

Introduction

The climate map of Théxar is determined by three factors: the low insolation of the K2V star, the elevated CO₂ content of the atmosphere, and the small axial tilt (14.2°). The result is smooth transitions between zones and the absence of sharp seasonal contrasts. This chapter details the climate zonation introduced in → see [Volume I, Ch.15, §15.6].

7.1 The Equatorial Zone (0°–12°)

Parameter Value
Average temperature 60.3–62.5°X
Precipitation 2,500–4,000 mm/year
Humidity 75–95%
T amplitude (annual) ±2.1°X

Characterization: Humid tropical forests covering the southern coast of Kharak and the northern one of Rhuval. No dry season; precipitation is distributed evenly. Biodiversity is maximal — up to 12,000 plant species per 10 mo².

Key regions:

  • The Thar Delta (Kharak) — 0.03 million mo² of jungle
  • Northern Rhuval — 0.08 million mo² of hylea

7.2 The Tropical Zone (12°–28°)

Parameter Value
Average temperature 54.9–60.3°X
Precipitation 800–2,000 mm/year (seasonal)
Humidity 50–70%
Dry season 3–5 months

Characterization: Savannas and open woodlands. Seasonality is weakly expressed because of the small axial tilt. Grassland communities with islands of forest dominate.

Key regions:

  • The Central Plain of Kharak — 0.34 million mo²
  • Savannas of Rhuval — 0.21 million mo²

7.3 The Subtropical Zone (28°–38°)

Parameter Value
Average temperature 51.6–55.9°X
Precipitation 500–1,200 mm/year
Humidity 40–60%

Characterization: Mediterranean-type climate. Warm, dry summers; mild, wet winters. The most favorable zone for agriculture.

Key regions:

  • The coast of the Serrin Sea (Kharak)
  • The western coast of Rhuval
  • The northern coast of Vexar

7.4 The Temperate Zone (38°–54°)

Parameter Value
Average temperature 47.3–52.6°X
Precipitation 500–900 mm/year
Seasonal amplitude ±8.2°X

Characterization: Broadleaf and mixed forests. A clear division into four seasons, but the temperature swing is small. The main settlement zone of the Xha'Ruun.

Key regions:

  • Southeastern Kharak — 0.41 million mo² of forest
  • Central Vexar — 0.28 million mo²

7.5 The Subpolar Zone (54°–66°)

Parameter Value
Average temperature 42.4–47.3°X
Precipitation 200–500 mm/year
Snow cover 4–6 months

Characterization: Coniferous forests (taiga) and tundra. Low population density. The main source of forest resources.

Key regions:

  • The northern taiga of Kharak — 0.47 million mo²
  • The southern tundras of Rhuval — 0.12 million mo²

7.6 The Polar Zone (66°–90°)

Parameter Value
Average temperature 31.5–42.4°X
Precipitation 50–200 mm/year
Ice cover Permanent

Characterization: Polar deserts and ice caps. Population — only research stations. Area of the polar caps — 0.17 million mo² (northern) and 0.13 million mo² (southern).

Research stations:

  • Ven-Deep (North Pole) — 800 people
  • Khar-Ice (South Pole) — 400 people

7.7 Altitudinal Zonation

The climate zones repeat vertically. For every 305 sha of height the temperature falls by ~2.0°X.

Belt Height (sha) Climatic analogue
Plain 0–610 Equatorial–tropical
Foothill 610–1,829 Subtropical
Mountain 1,829–3,659 Temperate
Subalpine 3,659–5,488 Subpolar
Alpine 5,488–7,317 Polar
Nival >7,317 Glaciers

Example: the Khar-Tor ridge includes all belts from tropical to nival. The highest point — Mt. Verksan (9,537 sha) — is covered with ice year-round.

7.8 The Influence of the Oceans

The Thalas and Gharra oceans smooth the climatic contrasts:

Factor Thalas (Northern) Gharra (Southern)
Water temperature (eq.) 62.5°X 60.3°X
Water temperature (pol.) 44.0°X 42.0°X
Influence on climate Softening of winters on Kharak Moderate influence on Rhuval

The cold Thel current along the eastern coast of Kharak creates a drier climate. The warm Khar-Vex current along the western coast intensifies precipitation.

Chapter Summary

  1. Seven climate zones: from the equatorial (62.5°X) to the polar (31.5°X).
  2. The 14.2° axial tilt smooths seasonal contrasts.
  3. Altitudinal zonation repeats the latitudinal one.
  4. The oceans moderate the climate; currents create local differences.
  5. The polar caps — 0.30 million mo², population — research stations.
