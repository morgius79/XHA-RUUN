Volume VIII: Atlas — Chapter 9: Political Domains

"Borders are not walls. Borders are agreements. Eight domains divide the land, but they do not divide the sky." — Charter of the Council of Unity, +850 Y.E.

Related sections: → see [Volume III, Ch.05, "The 8 Domains"], → see [Volume II, Ch.11, "History of the Eight Domains"], → see [Volume I, Ch.13, "Continents"], → see [Volume III, Ch.02, "The Council of Unity"] Length: 15 pages

Introduction

The eight domains cover the entire territory of Théxar. The domain borders reflect historical, cultural, and economic differences formed over millennia. This chapter details the territorial structure described in → see [Volume III, Ch.05] and → see [Volume II, Ch.11].

9.1 Kharak-Thar (Domain of Light-River)

Parameter Value

Geographical borders:

  • North: the coast of the Serrin Sea (42°N – 62°N)

Economic specialization: administrative center, science, high technology, education. The domain's GDP — 24% of the planetary. Guild headquarters: Scholars, Keepers, Scribes.

Largest cities:

  • Ven-Khal — 4.2 million
  • Thar-Deep — 2.8 million

Research stations:

  • Ven-Deep (North Pole) — climatology, glaciology
  • Khar-Ice (South Pole) — astronomy, geophysics

Economic specialization: scientific research, climate monitoring. The domain's budget — grant-based (1% of the planetary science budget).

Feature: the only domain without a permanent civilian population. The personnel works in shifts (3–12 months).

Legal status: zones of inter-species consent (→ see [Volume I, Ch.40, "The Three Species of Théxar"]). Decisions require the approval of representatives of all species.

Chapter Summary

  1. Eight domains: 3 on Kharak, 2 on Rhuval, 2 on Vexar, 1 island, 1 polar.
  2. Total population — ~2.2 billion (Xha'ri-primary).
  3. Kharak-Thar is the largest domain (480 million), the Polar the smallest (12 thousand).
  4. Representation in the Council — 25 seats, distributed with population taken into account.
  5. Inter-species territories — zones of consent of the three sentient species.
