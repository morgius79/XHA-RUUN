23.5 Climate Role

23.5.1 Albedo

Cryosphere reflects ~8% incoming radiation. No sheets → albedo 0.31→0.28 → warming ~0.8°X.

23.5.2 Fresh Water

Ice sheets 202 k mo³ = 68% fresh water. Groundwater 70.5 k mo³, rivers+lakes 1 510 mo³.

23.5.3 Paleoclimate

S sheet core (4 390 sha) → continuous 500 k cycle record (T, atm composition, volcanism).
