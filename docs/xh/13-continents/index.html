Kharak (root khar- — "light", "star") — largest continent, ancient civilization core. First Empire origin, capital, majority industry + science.

13.2.1 Relief ven Geology

Geologically oldest — craton ~5.5 bln cycle. 3 main zone:

  • Central Plain — lowland (0.34 M mo²), elevation 244–732 sha ASL. Ancient river deposit → main agricultural breadbasket
  • Khar-Tor Ridge — western coast range, ~976 mo, Mt. Verksan (9 537 sha, highest on Théxar). Active orogenesis, rich ore
  • Shan Desert — SE rocky plateau (0.14 M mo²). Gravel + remnant ancient rock. Rich U + Th

Rhuval (root rhuun — "people", "community") — 2nd largest, meridional orientation → wide climate range.

13.3.1 Relief ven Geology

Formed from Vaal-Kharak breakup ~2.5 bln cycle-tor. Younger + more active:

  • Raal Plateau — central highland (1 463–3 415 sha ASL), Prototectonic mafic intrusion. Richest Pt, Cr, Mn
  • Irkhen Mountain Chain — N coast volcanic ridge. Mt. Moraat (7 915 sha). Volcanism above planetary avg
  • Thash-Ven Valley — fertile alluvial plain

13.3.3 Economics

~35% GDP. Mining (Pt, Au, Ag, Cr), heavy industry, petrochem. Largest industrial + port center. Military tradition.

Rass-Ven: Only continent with active volcanism threatening populated area. Last major eruption (Irkhen, +3 890 y.c.) → 2 M temporarily evacuated. Best volcano monitoring on planet.

Vexar (root vex- — "give", "create") — smallest, most isolated. Separated from Rhuval by Ven-Khal Strait (85 mo narrowest).

13.4.1 Isolation ven Endemism

Vexar-Rhuval separation ~1.0 bln cycle-tor → isolated development:

  • 31% endemic plant vs Kharak 12%, Rhuval 18%
  • Unique forest ecosystem — silicon-enriched plant dominant
  • No large terrestrial predator (small pack species-mor)

13.4.2 Cultural

  • Philosophical "Third Way" school (independent from Kharak guild)
  • Own architectural tradition (local black basalt)
  • 15% territory = "Silence Zones" (Law of Silence)

13.4.3 Economics

~15% GDP. REE (Ti, Mn, Cr, Co), biopharmaceutical, quantum computing, tourism. Research institute + biotech laboratory hub.

13.6 Ecological Zone

Axial tilt 14.2° (lesser latitude diversity), still rich palette from:

  1. Elevation variation — coastal → >8 537 sha
  2. Rain shadow — Shan Desert = Khar-Tor rain shadow
  3. Ocean current — warm Khar-Vex (Kharak W), cold Thel (E)
  4. Geothermal oasis — Rhuval volcanic plateau
