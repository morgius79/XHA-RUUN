Ruun 22: Xa-Vek Sha-Ven — Hydrology

"Rivers are the blood vessels of the planet. They carry life from the mountains to the oceans and back, in the clouds. To stop a river is to stop the heart of Théxar." — Book of the Cycle, Xha'Ruun
