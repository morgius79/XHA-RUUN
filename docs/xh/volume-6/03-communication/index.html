Thexar-VI: Khal-Vex-El — Sha-El 03: Xha-Ven Khal-sha — Communication: The Biosphere Network

"Electricity is a crude, primitive way to transmit information. Thousands of years of evolution created the perfect network — mycelium. We simply learned to speak its language." — First Keeper of the Network, +2 800 Y.E.

Ruun-ak rass: → rhu [Thexar-VI, Sha-El 01, "Thel-ak Khal-vex"] Volume: 30 sha-ruun Mor-ven: Xha'Ruun do not use electromagnetic waves. They grew the Xha-Ven biosphere network — a planetary mycelial organism at depths of 500–2 000 m, connected into a single organism (Rass-Mor, not sentient, an ideal transmission medium). Information is encoded by the state of quantum entanglement in the hyphae.

3.1 Living Network (Xha-Ven)

Parameter Xha-Ven
Transmission speed Instantaneous (quantum entanglement)
Bandwidth ~10²⁴ bit/s
Coverage 100% land, 60% ocean (surface)
Security Absolute (data is part of the organism)
Energy consumption Powered by geothermal heat
Network size ~12 billion km of hyphae

A change in one part of the network is instantly reflected in all others — regardless of distance.

3.2 Crystal Memory (Ven-Khal)

Information is stored in the lattice defects of grown silicon-carbide crystals.

Parameter Ven-Khal crystal
Capacity 10¹⁸ TB (1 cm³)
Durability >10⁹ years (at room T)
Read energy 0 (optical reading)
Record destruction Only via nuclear decay

Read by passing H₂S laser light through the crystal; defects distort the light, information is reconstructed. A single 1 cm³ crystal holds all knowledge ever produced by humanity — repeated 10 000 times.

3.3 Emotional Transmission (Sha-Ven)

A technology that transmits not just information but sensory-emotional experience — total immersion into the consciousness of another being. Applications: education (living through a great engineer's experience), diplomacy, art, memory (preserving the personality of the deceased — but not restoring it, a philosophical prohibition).

3.4 Moryn Language — Without Technology

Moryn communicate through bioluminescent patterns visible to depths of 3 000 m; a single flash can convey information equivalent to 10 000 words. Translation uses living translators — modified organisms symbiotizing with both species.
