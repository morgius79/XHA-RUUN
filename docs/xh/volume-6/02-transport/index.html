Thexar-VI: Khal-Vex-El — Sha-El 02: Thar-Vex Khal-sha — Quasi-Transport and Resonance Networks

"Movement is an illusion. You do not move through space — you change your position in the planet's resonance lattice. Space is not a medium. Space is a relationship." — "Theory of Resonance Lattices," Khar-Vexar Academy, +3 200 Y.E.

Ruun-ak rass: → rhu [Thexar-VI, Sha-El 01, "Thel-ak Khal-vex"] Volume: 30 sha-ruun Mor-ven: Xha'Ruun do not use "transport." They use gravitational resonance corridors — stable curvatures of spacetime created by the resonance between Théxar, its moons, and Khar'Vex. An object synchronized with the planet's frequency does not spend energy on movement — it changes its position in the spatial lattice.

2.3 Orbital Elevator — Not an Elevator

2.4 Moryn Deep Currents

The Moryn use baro-resonance currents — movement through pressure differentials created by seismic activity. Surface to the bottom of the Thash trench (9 847 m) takes ~20 minutes, via an acoustic lens.
