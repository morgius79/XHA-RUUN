4.3 Crystal Memory (Ven-Khal)

Ven-Khal crystals are used as associative "dictionaries" for bio-colonies: a colony absorbs a crystal's information instantaneously in a single flash of light.

4.4 Limitations

  • Cannot operate in a vacuum
  • Sensitive to radiation (despite 5× Xha'Ruun resilience)
  • "Forget" information if not "fed" light of a specific spectrum
