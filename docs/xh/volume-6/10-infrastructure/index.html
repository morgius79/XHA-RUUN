Thexar-VI: Khal-Vex-El — Sha-El 10: Thel-El Khal-sha — Infrastructure

"The network is not roads and not wires. The network is what connects the desire of one with the possibility of another. Infrastructure is the body of society. A sick body cannot desire." — Guild of Engineers, "The Body of Society," +3 100 Y.E.

Ruun-ak rass: → rhu [Thexar-VI, Sha-El 02, "Thar-Vex Khal-sha"], → rhu [Thexar-VI, Sha-El 03, "Xha-Ven Khal-sha"], → rhu [Thexar-VI, Sha-El 09, "Vex-Thel Khal-sha"], → rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 03, "Khal-Ven Rass"] Volume: 20 sha-ruun Mor-ven: Infrastructure is the least visible and most important of technologies: it distributes energy, water, information, and food so that their absence becomes an event. The main lines of civilization are not visible — energy through thermonuclear circuits, information through mycelium, movement through resonance corridors. This chapter describes the six networks and how they form a single organism.

10.2 The Water Network

Hydrosphere 67% of surface; water distributed by living conduits — walls grown from Thar-Vex, populated with filtering organisms; water purified en route.

System Volume Note
Living conduits 9 billion m³/y 640 000 km, mycelial channels
Desalination colonies 1.2 billion m³/y Coastal, hybrid bio-filters
Underground reservoirs 240 billion m³ Nest-warehouses
Atmospheric condensers 0.6 billion m³/y Shan Desert, highlands

The right to water is vested in every living being regardless of species.

10.5 The Waste Network and the Closed Cycle

Stream Volume Recycling
Industrial waste 420 M t/y 96.2%
Biological waste 890 M t/y 100% (composting)
Spent building materials 210 M t/y 96.4%

Orbital "shepherds" — young Xha-Vokh that collect spent satellites; thanks to them the orbital environment remains clean. The closure is a historical imperative: the civilization survived ecological collapse (−2 150) and has no intention of repeating it (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 04, "Thar-Xa-Khal"]).

10.7 Life Support of Settlements

Autonomous for 90–120 days (covering crisis periods of any network):

System Coverage Reserve
Food 100% local farms 90 d
Water 100% 120 d
Energy 100% (resonance + local) 90 d

The principle is redundancy through diversity: not one giant system but six independent ones, deliberately weakly connected — a legacy of the First Empire's collapse with its centralized infrastructure.
