−6 150 (EV-0004) — Verksaren I (CH-0001), Thar Valley clan leader, united city-states (2 decades of campaigns). Territory at founding ~2.8 M km² (all Kharak); later 2 continents.

Union by legal project, not force: Imperial Code offered common norm vs divergent customs. Law, not fear, held Empire 9 centuries.

3.1.2 Imperial Code

1st body of laws, Verksaren I. 48 articles, 42 survive.

  1. Equal for equal — restoration, not vengeance (softened talion)
  2. Community responsibility for member's crime (collective suretyship)
  3. Inviolability of envoys + merchants — trade legal base
  4. Farmer protection from tax collectors
  5. Slave right to buy freedom; slaves' children free
  6. Religious cult freedom (if imperial cult recognized)

Predecessor: Code of Khar (≈−7 200). Extended unified norm across Kharak.

3.2.2 Settlement of Rhuval

−5 600: 1st permanent Xha'ri settlements on Rhuval. By −4 000 integral part of Empire (internal autonomy: local dynasties under emperor). Annexation doubled resource base → cultural flourishing.

3.3.2 Restricted Slavery (Code-limited)

  • Right to buy freedom (fixed sum)
  • Inviolability of life — killing slave = killing free person
  • Slaves' children born free — not inherited
  • Term — debt slavery ≤10 y

Harsher than other planets' forms? No — milder. Fully abolished Era of Unity +1 500.

3.4 Cultural Flourishing (−4 500 – −3 000)

  • Architecture: basalt palaces/temples, monumental acoustic halls
  • Literature: Verksaren deeds poems, first chronicles
  • Science: six-base math, astronomy (Serrin/Thel), medicine
  • Law: Imperial Code commentaries — legal tradition birth

Largest monument: Hall of the First (Council Hall), Khar-Vexar — acoustics for collective singing (resonator temple tradition, → rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 10]).

3.5 End of Era — Crisis Accumulation

By −3 000 peak reached; imbalances:

  • Demographic pressure — Kharak overpopulation, depleted ag resources
  • Bureaucratization — admin apparatus growth
  • Rhuval regional autonomy — center/periphery tension
  • Ecological footprint — deforestation, soil erosion

Fall −2 150 (ecocollapse + epidemic). Not external conquest — internal exhaustion of resource model.
