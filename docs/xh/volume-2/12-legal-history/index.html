Thexar-II Sha-El 12: Khal-Sha The-El — The Legal History

"Law is a wall that protects the weak from the strong. When the wall crumbles — chaos follows. When the wall is too high — stagnation follows." — From the "Treatise on Law", +2 000 E.E.

Ruun-ak rass: → rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 03, "Verksan The-El"], → rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 08, "Xa-Sha-Ven"], → rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 06, "Rass-Vokh Thar-El"] Volume: 25 sha-ruun Mor-ven: Custom → codified law → constitutional system regulating relations between three sapient species. Each era left a legal layer built into the next: Imperial Code principles outlived the Empire, treaty law → Council Charter basis, modern law absorbed all that preceded. Five stages. Law of Accord: Xha'ri, Kel'vash, Moryn.

12.1 Stages of Legal Development

Era System Basis
Pre-state Customary law Precedents, traditions
First Empire Imperial Code Codification
Dark ages Regional law Divergent systems
Renaissance Treaty law Unity Treaty
Era of Unity Constitutional law Council Charter

Each transition = response to crisis of previous system: codification → uncertainty of custom; treaty law → fragmentation; constitutional law → complexity of intercontinental society.

12.2 Customary Law

Pre-state era: law = custom, transmitted orally, applied by precedent, interpreted by elders of the kin.

  • Collective responsibility — the community answered for its members
  • Restitution, not vengeance — harm compensated, blood feud restrained
  • Sacral status — norm violations seen as insult to the spirits

Code of Khar (≈−7 200, 40 articles) = first written body fixing these customs (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 02, "Xha-Sha Vokh-El"]).

12.3 The Imperial Code

Created by Verksaren I (−6 150). 48 articles, of which 42 survive.

Key principles:

  • Equal for equal — restitution, not vengeance
  • Community responsibility — collective suretyship
  • Inviolability of envoys and merchants
  • The right of a slave to buy freedom
  • Freedom of slaves' children
  • Protection of the farmer from tax collector arbitrariness

First empire-wide legal act, spreading unified norm across all Kharak. Principles outlived the Empire: after fall (−2 150) surviving copies became core of legal tradition (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 03, "Verksan The-El"]).

12.4 Legal Decline in the Dark Ages

After the fall, unified law broke into regional systems. Loss of Imperial Code for 600 years (preserved only in the Irkhen Library) → return to customary law and arbitrary local norms. This period entrenched the value of written law as a guarantee against arbitrariness.

12.5 Treaty Law of the Renaissance

Unity Treaty (−750) — new legal construction: a treaty between equals — eight regions voluntarily limited autonomy in exchange for common guarantees. Key innovations:

  • Free trade as a treaty norm
  • Unified system of measures and weights
  • Renunciation of wars between participants
  • The Interregional Council — the first supranational legal body

12.6 Modern Law: The Council Charter

Based on the Charter of the Council of Unity (+850, with amendments):

Principle Significance
Equality of all species Xha'ri, Kel'vash, Moryn — equal before the law
Freedom of information Access to any fact of the canon
Inviolability of history Prohibition of rewriting
Rational stewardship The Law of Silence

Supplemented by the Law of Accord: no decision affecting all species without consultation of Xha'ri, Kel'vash, Moryn (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 14]). Justice on the basis of codified canon; interpretation controlled by the Keeper of Knowledge.
