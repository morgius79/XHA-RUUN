Thexar-II Sha-El 01: Xha'ri Thar-Vokh — Pre-Civilization Era

"Before the first words were written, before the first walls were raised, before the first fire was tamed — we were already here. We looked at the stars and knew: we are not alone in the universe, but there are no others like us." — Book of Origins, canonical, Era of Unity

Species formed ~15 000 y-tor on Central Plain Kharak. Civilization (settled, surplus, inter-generational knowledge) born in great river valleys.

1.2 Way of Life

1.2.1 Economy

1.2.2 Social Structure

Basic unit = kin group (20–50, common ancestor + shared economy).

  • Division by age, not sex — all 3 sexes equal in hunting/gathering/fishing
  • No property inequality — shared resources
  • Egalitarian kinship — offspring belong to group; no "father/mother" concept
  • Ritual status of elders — led rituals, kept oral tradition

Exchange networks: obsidian + shells hundreds of km from source. Contacts rare, non-hostile.

1.2.3 Spiritual Culture

Rock paintings, S Vexar caves (Ven-Shar). Stable themes:

  • 3-eyed figures — Xha'ri themselves, species identity awareness
  • Animals — Ghal-Shar, Shar-Ghar, wounded/pursuit scenes
  • Astronomical — Serrin + Thel configurations; phase cyclicity understood (prototype calendar)

Significance beyond "hunting magic" — recurring motifs over millennia → stable mythology (→ rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 10, "Vokh-Ven Sha-El"]).

1.3 Kharru Domestication

~−10 200, independently in 2 regions: Thar Valley (Kharak) + Raal Plateau (Rhuval).

Stage Time Description
Wild hunting −15 000 – −10 000 Main quarry
Taming −10 200 – −9 000 Cubs in settlements
Semi-domestication −9 000 – −8 000 Controlled herding
Full domestication −8 000 Captive breeding

Provides: meat, milk (dense populations), hides, workpower (hauling, early soil loosening). Parallel domestication of single species in 2 distant regions — kharru was biologically "ready" (social, docile, herding).

1.5 End of Era — Boundary with Early Civilization

Era ends −9 780 — proto-writing invented (EV-0002), independently in 3 regions (Thar Valley Kharak, Rhuval coast, S Vexar).

Why writing = boundary:

  • Documented history begins (beyond oral tradition)
  • Accounting + accumulation → economy beyond immediate consumption
  • 3 independent centers → societies dense/complex enough to need recording

Final era achievements: permanent settlement (Thar-Vex −11 500), domesticated kharru (−10 200), proto-agriculture. Missing: the tool of memory — writing.
