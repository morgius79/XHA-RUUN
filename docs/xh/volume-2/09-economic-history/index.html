Thexar-II Sha-El 09: Khal-Ven The-El — The Economic History

"From the hoe to the fusion reactor — a path of 12,000 years. From barter to post-scarcity — a path of 4,000 years. Economics is the history of our freedom." — Guild of Merchants, economic treatise, +3 800

Ruun-ak rass: → rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 03, "Verksan The-El"], → rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 09, "Thar-Vek Rass-Ven"], → rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 08, "Xa-Vokh The-El"] Volume: 25 sha-ruun Mor-ven: Successive overcoming of scarcity: appropriative → post-scarcity. Each transition linked to a technological shift + changed social structure. Seven economic eras. Key transitions: agriculture −9 800, metallurgy −5 500, industrialization −1 200, fusion +1 420. Modern model: scarcity problem replaced by distribution problem.

9.1 The Eras of the Economy

Era Period Character Main resource
Appropriative −15 000 – −9 800 Hunting, gathering Game, fruits
Agrarian −9 800 – −5 500 Farming, livestock Grain, meat
Craft −5 500 – −2 150 Metals, pottery Copper, bronze

Era boundaries = technological shifts: agriculture (−9 800), metallurgy (−5 500), industrial revolution (−1 200, steam engine), fusion energy (+1 420). Economy always outran the political system — institutions (Empire, Treaty, Council) arose in response to economic needs.

9.2 Key Transitions

9.2.1 From Appropriation to Production (−9 800)

Agriculture + domestication (kharru, ~−10 200) → surplus — economic foundation of civilization. First accumulations → written accounting (−9 780) and cities (−7 800).

9.2.2 The Imperial Economy (−6 150 – −2 150)

First integrated economy: unified measures, roads, tax system, division of labor between regions. Fall = limit of extensive growth: imperial model exhausted Central Plain soils, destroyed its own resource base (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 04, "Thar-Xa-Khal"]).

9.2.3 Industrialization (−1 200)

Steam engine (Thal-Ven, Shan-En, −1 200) began the industrial era. Electrification (−240) + subsequent automation completed the transition craft → industrial.

9.2.4 Post-Scarcity (+1 420 – present)

Controlled fusion (+1 420): energy practically inexhaustible and cheap. + automation of extraction and production → post-scarcity economy: basic resources ceased to be limited.

9.3 The Post-Scarcity Economy

  • Basic resources (energy, food, housing) — available to everyone without payment
  • Handcrafted and artisanal goods — market-based (prestige goods)
  • Labor — voluntary, organized on the guild principle
  • Planetary GDP: 124 trillion solar units
  • GDP per capita: 44 000 s.u.

Key features:

  1. Near-zero cost for basic goods — distributed by need, not purchasing power
  2. The market survives for differentiated goods — artisanal products, works of art, unique services
  3. Motivation shifts from survival to meaning — work = voluntary contribution to guild and society, not necessity
  4. The Growth Commandment limits demographic expansion so resource base stays sustainable

9.4 The Role of the Guilds in the Economy

Economic structure determined by 12 guilds (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 08, "Xa-Vokh The-El"]):

  • Production and services — guild principle (Engineers, Agronomists, Builders, Miners, Navigators)
  • Distribution and trade — Merchants Guild
  • Accounting and administration — Scribes Guild
  • Knowledge and science — Scholars and Keepers guilds

Guilds = simultaneously economic actors and regulators: set quality standards + ensure their observance.
