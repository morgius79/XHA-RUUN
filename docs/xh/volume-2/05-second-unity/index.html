5.1 Unity Treaty (−750)

8 Kharak regions signed in Khar-Vexar (Ven-Rass-Dok).

Terms:

  1. Free trade between regions
  2. Unified measures/weights
  3. Free citizen movement
  4. Renunciation of interregional war (Great Truce)
  5. Joint external defense
  6. Unified calendar (0 y.e.)
  7. Interregional Council — Council of Unity prototype

Key: coordinated, not abolished, regional power. Regions kept self-government, delegated trade/measures/defense. Federation of autonomies — civilizational hallmark for millennia.

5.2 Cultural Renaissance (−600 – −200) — "Age of Enlightenment"

5.2.1 Philosophy — "School of the Three Paths"

  • Path of Knowledge (Ven) — truth through science
  • Path of Harmony (Rass) — balance with nature
  • Path of Action (Khal) — transformation of world

Complementary triad: knowledge w/o action barren, action w/o harmony destructive, harmony w/o knowledge blind. → basis for Teaching of Vibration + Council ethics.

5.3.2 Electrification

By +200 electricity = main industry energy (displaced steam). Social dimension: standardization + centralized management → engineering guilds stronger → planetary infrastructure.

5.4 Unification of Continents

  • Rhuval: permanent diplomatic+trade ties after Treaty → joined −200 (2nd continent)
  • Vexar: joined +200 after regular Gharra sea route → Treaty covers all 3 continents, effectively planetary

5.5 Preconditions of Council of Unity

By +500: regional system can't handle intercontinental economy.

Problems: differing tax systems, guild trade disputes, no unified labor law, orbital infrastructure coordination.

Solution: +800 negotiations → +850 founding of Council of Unity (Rass-Vokh-Sha) — 49 members (24 guilds, 25 regions), supreme representative body. → new era (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 06]).
