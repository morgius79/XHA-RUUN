Thexar-II Sha-El 16: Xha-Mor Thar-El — The Demographic History

"A people is not numbers. But numbers tell the story of a people." — Guild of Scribes, demographic report, +4 000 Y.E.

Key patterns:

  • First Empire peak (−2 150): ~18 M (Kharak) — the limit at which Central Plain agricultural resources were exhausted
  • Crisis (−750): decline to ~4 M — demographic collapse following the ecological one
  • Recovery and Era of Unity: growth accelerates with each technological transition (industrialization −1 200, fusion +1 420, post-scarcity +2 800)

Xha'ri remain the majority, but their share gradually declines due to higher Kel'vash growth. Demographic policy considers all three species: Growth Commandment applies to total population, Council monitors the balance (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 14, "Xha-Sha Xha-Mor"]).

16.3 The Growth Commandment

Modern demographic regime regulated by the Growth Commandment: growth must not exceed 0.3% per year. Mechanism = cultural norm + economic planning, not coercion:

  • Family policy encourages stability rather than maximizing births
  • Education and medicine make family planning accessible
  • Post-scarcity economy removes economic incentives for large families

Direct response to the ecological crisis of the First Empire (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 04, "Thar-Xa-Khal"]): the civilization consciously limits numbers so as not to repeat the mistake of exhausting the resource base.
