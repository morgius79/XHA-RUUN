  • Thermonuclear (+1 420 – +2 000): fusion, end of fossil fuels, 1st orbital stations; Th + He-3 (→ rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 33, "Fusion"] )

  • Abundance (+2 800 – present): post-scarcity, full automation, moon exploration (Thel base); economics shifts scarcity → distribution
