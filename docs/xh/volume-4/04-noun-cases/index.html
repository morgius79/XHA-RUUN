Thexar-IV: The-Sha-Ruun — Sha-El 04: The-El Sha-Thar — 14 Cases

"14 cases — 14 questions that can be asked of an object. From where? To where? Where? With what? With whom? About what? The Xha'Ruun language does not allow one to remain in ignorance." — Grammar Reference, Khar-Vexar Academy

Ruun-ak rass: → rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 03, "Khal-sha Rass"], → rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 05, "Verbs and Tense"] Volume: 25 sha-ruun Mor-ven: One of the most complete case systems among known languages: 14 cases cover grammatical, spatial, temporal relations. Agglutinative suffixes attach after the number suffix → transparent marking. Split ergativity — a typological rarity. Spatial system distinguishes rest, direction, source.

4.1 Case System

No. Case Suffix Question Example
1 Nominative -∅ Who? What? xha'ruun — people
2 Ergative -sha Who? (agent) xha'ruun-sha — people (acts)
3 Accusative -thar Whom? What? xha'ruun-thar — people (I see)
4 Dative -ven To whom? To what? xha'ruun-ven — to the people
5 Genitive -mor Whose? xha'ruun-mor — of the people
6 Instrumental -khal With what? xha'ruun-khal — by/with the people
7 Comitative -rass With whom? xha'ruun-rass — together with the people
8 Ablative -tor From where? xha'ruun-tor — from the people
9 Inessive -the Where? (inside) xha'ruun-the — in the people
10 Superessive -vex Where? (on) xha'ruun-vex — on the people
11 Allative -khar To where? xha'ruun-khar — toward the people
12 Translative -el Become what? xha'ruun-el — (turn into) the people
13 Essive -en Be what? xha'ruun-en — (as) the people
14 Terminative -mor-thal Until when? xha'ruun-mor-thal — up to the people

4.2 Grammatical Cases

Four cases mark the grammatical role:

  • Nominative (-∅) — the subject of an intransitive verb
  • Ergative (-sha) — the agent of a transitive verb
  • Accusative (-thar) — the patient of a transitive verb
  • Dative (-ven) — the addressee

Ergative strategy (effective) combines with the nominative for intransitive verbs — a typologically rare "split ergativity".

4.3 Spatial Cases

Five cases localize an object:

  • Inessive (-the) — inside
  • Superessive (-vex) — on the surface
  • Ablative (-tor) — movement from
  • Allative (-khar) — movement to
  • Prolative (-the-mor) — movement through (compound)

The spatial system distinguishes rest, direction, and source.

4.4 Other Cases

  • Genitive (-mor) — possession
  • Instrumental (-khal) — instrument
  • Comitative (-rass) — accompaniment
  • Translative (-el) — transformation
  • Essive (-en) — state
  • Terminative (-mor-thal) — limit

4.5 Case and Number

Case suffixes attach after the number suffix: xha'ruun-ak-sha ("peoples-ERG"). The order is mandatory and reversible in analysis.
