Thexar-IV: The-Sha-Ruun — Sha-El 09: Mor-Ruun Khal-sha — Senary Numeral System

"Six is the foundation of everything. 6 limbs, 6 letters of the genetic code, 6 digits in our system. Six is the number of harmony." — Mathematical Treatise, +2 000 Y.E.

Ruun-ak rass: → rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 03, "Khal-sha Rass"], → rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 34, "Théxar-mor The-ven Khal-sha-ak"] Volume: 15 sha-ruun Mor-ven: Language and mathematics based on the senary numeral system. Base 6 motivated by biology (six limbs) and genetics (six-letter code). Not only a way of writing numbers but a cultural constant — reflected in the calendar, measurements, architecture. Digit names coincide with basic roots.

9.1 The Digits

Digit Symbol (Unicode) Name
0 U+E000 mor (void)
1 U+E001 ven (one)
2 U+E002 sha (two)
3 U+E003 tor (three)
4 U+E004 vex (four)
5 U+E005 rass (five)

Names coincide with the basic roots (→ rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 08, "Sha-Ven The"]): ven (truth), sha (language), tor (strength), vex (home), rass (accord) — numbers conceptually connected with cultural meanings.

9.2 Examples of Counting

Place value senary: "ven-mor" = 1×6 + 0 = 6. Compound numerals agglutinative — each place attached with the suffix -mor.

9.3 Senary Mathematics

Defines the mathematical culture:

  • Fractions — positional, in powers of 6
  • Calendar — a year of 412.7 days, 14 months (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 17, "Thar-Mor The-El"])
  • Measurements — units of length, mass, and time are multiples of 6 (→ rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 34, "Théxar-mor The-ven Khal-sha-ak"])

9.4 Cultural Significance

The number 6 is a cultural constant: 6 limbs, 6 letters of the code, 6 digits, 6 spatial cases, 6 laws of the canon. Senary harmony pervades language, science, and religion.
