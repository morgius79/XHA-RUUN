Thexar-IV: The-Sha-Ruun — Sha-El 12: Mor-El — Conclusion

"Language is not merely a means of communication. Language is a way of thinking. Xha'Ruun grammar — with its 14 cases and mandatory evidentiality — is no accident. It is a reflection of how our people see the world: precisely, honestly, responsibly." — Keeper of Knowledge, +4 028 Y.E.

Ruun-ak rass: → rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 01–11], → rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 06, "Rass-Vokh Thar-El"], → rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 07, "Xha-Thar-El"] Volume: 10 sha-ruun Mor-ven: Volume IV described the Xha'Ruun language from sounds to the numeral system: phonetics and phonology, grammar with 14 cases, the verbal system, mandatory evidentiality, the Sharuu writing system, the lexicon, the senary numeral system, dialects, and the history of the language. The conclusion brings these descriptions into a single picture and defines the place of the language in culture and history.

Key Ideas

  1. Precision — 14 cases and mandatory evidentiality allow expressing an exact picture of the world: who speaks, where they know from, how it is connected (→ rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 04, "The-El Sha-Thar"], → rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 06, "Sha-Ven Rass"]).
  2. Agglutination — the lexicon grows not by borrowing but by combining roots; ~500 roots generate 50 000+ words (→ rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 08, "Sha-Ven The"]).
  3. Unity in diversity — a single standard and five mutually intelligible dialects; the language unites without unifying (→ rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 10, "Shaven Xha-Mor"]).
  4. Writing as memory — Sharuu (124 syllabic signs + 48 logograms) preserved the language through centuries of crises (→ rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 07, "Sharuu Khal-sha"]).
  5. Six as a constant — the senary system was reflected in mathematics, the calendar, and measurements (→ rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 09, "Mor-Ruun Khal-sha"]).

Language and History

The modern language is the result of the processing of historical experience (→ rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 11, "The-Sha-Ruun The-El"]). Imperial standardization laid the foundations of the grammar; the crisis gave rise to dialectal diversity; the Unity Treaty returned the norm. The language survived all political catastrophes precisely because it was written down: writing became a "repository" that survived where institutions collapsed (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 04, "Thar-Xa-Khal"]).

Looking Ahead

The language faces new challenges:

  • Stellar expansion — will the unity of the language survive the settlement of other stars?
  • Interspecies communication — how do the languages of the three species coexist (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 14, "Xha-Sha Xha-Mor"])?
  • The digital age — how will the Sharuu writing system change in digital systems (→ rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 07, "Sharuu Khal-sha"])?

The answers to these questions will be determined by the next chapter of history (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 07, "Xha-Thar-El"]).
