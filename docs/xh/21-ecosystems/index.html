21.3 Unique Ecosystems

21.3.1 Equatorial Jungle (0°–8° lat, S Kharak, N Rhuval)

5-layer structure:

Layer Height Closure Species Light
A (emergent) 60–85 m <10% 120 Full
B (canopy) 30–60 m 85% 2 800 Partial
C (understory) 5–30 m 60% 4 200 Heavy shade
D (shrub) 1–5 m 30% 3 800 Low
E (floor) 0–1 m <5% 1 200 Minimal

Adaptation: Lower-layer leaves absorb H₂S directly from air (stomata) → metabolic compensation for low light.
