Xha'Ruun-Vokh: Gharra Rass (The Deep Covenant)

Time: +2 000 Y.E.

Moryn-ak gharra-the en-ak-ven. Moryn-PL depth-INESSIVE be-PAST-DIR "The Moryn dwell in the depths."

El-ak thal-thar rass-en-ven. They-PL water-ACC harmony-PRS-DIR "They live in harmony with the water."

Ven-Khal-sha el-ak-rass sha-ak-ven. Ven-Khal-ERG they-PL-COM speak-PAST-DIR "Ven-Khal spoke with them."

El-ak-sha rass-mor vex-ak-ven. They-PL-ERG silence give-PAST-DIR "They gave the gift of silence."

Glossary:

  • Moryn-ak = Moryn (plural)
  • gharra-the = in the depths (gharra = deep ocean, -the = inessive)
  • thal-thar = water (accusative)
  • rass-en-ven = are in harmony (rass = harmony, -en = present, -ven = direct)
  • el-ak-rass = with them (el = they, -ak = plural, -rass = comitative)
  • rass-mor = silence (harmony + time → "timeless" → silence)
