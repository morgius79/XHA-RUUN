12.1.3 Water Origin

Mixed:

  • ~60% mantle degassing (volcanic H₂O vapor)
  • ~30% icy planetesimal (outer system)
  • ~10% cometary

Elevated metallicity → more radioactive element in crust → slower cooling → longer volcanic degassing → significant "internal" H₂O (compensates smaller size).

12.2 Internal Structure

12.2.1 Crust

Thickness 2.4 mo (ocean) → 9.1 mo (continent), avg 7.1 mo.

  • Oceanic (~2.4–3.7 mo): basaltic, density ~2.8 kh/dL³. Rift-formed, subduction-recycled. Avg age 180 M cycle.
  • Continental (~6.1–9.1 mo): granite-metamorphic, density ~2.6 kh/dL³. Core age 5.5–5.0 bln cycle.

Rass-Ven: Continental crust Th anomaly ~2.5× standard (metallicity-mor). Xha'Ruun energy foundation (→ rhu [Ruun 09]).

12.2.2 Mantle

Silicate shell ~582 mo thick. T at core-mantle boundary ~109°X lower (smaller size, lower center pressure) → lower convection, larger cells, longer renewal cycle.

Asthenosphere (upper mantle) — partial-melt zone → magma chamber origin → volcano feed.

12.4.2 Oxygen Catastrophe

Rass-Ven: 4.0–3.8 bln cycle-tor. Slower photosynthesis evolution (reduced insolation) → gradual process.

Consequence:

  1. Anaerobic mass extinction
  2. Ocean Fe oxidation → BIF (banded iron formation)
  3. Ozone layer formation (UV protection)
  4. O₂ composition: trace → ~12%
