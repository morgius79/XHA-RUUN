Thexar-III: Ruun-El — Sha-El 10: Ven-Khal-mor The-El — Organization of Science

"Truth knows no borders. Science knows no domains. Knowledge belongs to all." — Scholars Guild, motto

Ruun-ak rass: → rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 10, "The-Ven Thar-El"], → rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 07, "The-Ven Khal-sha"], → rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 04, "Ruun-El Xha-Khal"] Volume: 20 sha-ruun Mor-ven: Science = institutional foundation of the civilization. Two principles: openness (knowledge belongs to all) and guild coordination (research managed by the Scholars Guild). Structure: 240 institutes, 48 universities, 36 observatories, 8 200 laboratories. R&D 7.2% of GDP; 8.4 M scientists. Ethical limits — a framework, not a prohibition.

10.1 Structure

Element Count Function
Institutes 240 Research
Universities 48 Education
Observatories 36 Astronomy
Laboratories 8 200 Applied science

Network distributed across all domains (→ rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 05, "Sha-El Xha-Thar"]), ensuring a diversity of scientific schools. Astronomical network includes orbital observatories and stations on the moons.

10.2 Budget

  • R&D spending: 7.2% of GDP
  • Scientists: 8.4 million (all species)
  • Priorities: energy, biotechnology, space

7.2% of GDP on R&D — among the highest of known civilizations. Funding does not depend on market conditions: fundamental research is supported as a basic value.

10.3 Guild Coordination

Organized through the Scholars Guild (→ rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 04, "Ruun-El Xha-Khal"]):

  • Program coordination — priorities and resource allocation
  • Publication — a unified open access system
  • Collegial review — review of the most important discoveries
  • Industry links — transfer of results to engineering, medicine, industry

Role of the Keeper Guild — archiving results and controlling canon conformity.

10.4 Interspecies Science

All three species participate (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 14, "Xha-Sha Xha-Mor"]): Kel'vash — engineering and geological directions, Moryn — oceanography and deep-sea biology. Interspecies coordination governed by the Law of Accord.

10.5 Ethical Limits

Limited by an ethical code:

  • Biological caution — restriction of risky research
  • Ethics of sapience — regulation of consciousness research
  • Limited AI — prohibition of self-aware artificial intelligence

Not a prohibition of knowledge but an institutional framework (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 10, "The-Ven Thar-El"]).
