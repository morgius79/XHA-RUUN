Thexar-III: Ruun-El — Sha-El 09: Vokh-Shan Khal-sha — The Law of Silence

"Silence is not the absence of sound. Silence is the absence of interference. There are places where nature must be left to itself." — From the Law of Silence, CANON.md §4.3

Origins: Vexar's ecological thought (first to create nature reserves) + catastrophic lesson of the First Empire. Principle: not "preservation for man" but recognition of nature's intrinsic value.

9.1 The Law

Certain zones declared technology-free reserves: industrial activity, extraction, construction, and other interference prohibited. The law protects not only individual species but entire ecosystems — from deep-sea to polar.

Principle: not "preservation for man" but recognition of nature's intrinsic value — ecosystems exist not to serve civilization but in their own right.

9.2 Protected Zones

Zone Area Type Prohibitions
Gharra Deep 512 thousand mo² Oceanic Industry, extraction
Vexar Rainforest 132 thousand mo² Forest Logging, construction
Khar-Tor Peaks 74 thousand mo² Mountain Building
Shan Core 24.8 thousand mo² Desert All activities
Ven-Deep (subglacial) 496 mo² Lake Drilling

Total — hundreds of thousands of km². Represent all major biomes: ocean, forest, mountains, desert, subglacial lakes.

9.3 Protection and Monitoring

Guarded by a system without technological interference:

  • Observation — remote monitoring without the presence of machinery
  • Scientific access — limited research programs
  • Protection — prevention of illegal activity

The principle of "Silence" extends to scientific research: observation is permitted, interference is not.

9.4 The Law of Silence in Culture

Not only a legal norm but also a cultural value:

  • Ecological education — a mandatory part of the school curriculum
  • Ritual significance — visiting protected zones without technology — a form of spiritual practice
  • Identity — the law inherits Vexar's ecological ethics
