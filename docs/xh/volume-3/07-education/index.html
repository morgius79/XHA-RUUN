Thexar-III: Ruun-El — Sha-El 07: The-Ven Khal-sha — Education

"Knowledge is the only property that multiplies when shared." — Keeper of Knowledge, founding of the Academy of Khar-Vexar, +1 200

Ruun-ak rass: → rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 01, "Rass-Vokh Khal-sha"], → rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 04, "Ruun-El Xha-Khal"], → rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 10, "The-Ven Thar-El"] Volume: 25 sha-ruun Mor-ven: Education = foundation of civilization, its highest institutional priority. Lesson of the dark ages (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 04, "Thar-Xa-Khal"]): knowledge is fragile, its transmission requires a system. Continuous system: primary → secondary → guild → academy. Coverage: literacy 100%, secondary 98%, guild 72%, academic degree 12%.

7.1 The Education System

Level Age Duration Content
Primary 5–10 years 5 years Writing, arithmetic, canon
Secondary 10–15 years 5 years Sciences, languages, guilds
Higher (Guild) 15–20 years 5 years Specialization
Academy 20+ 3–8 years Research work

Built on the principle of progressive narrowing: from universal primary education to specialized guild training. Secondary education introduces all 12 guilds' activities; choice of guild occurs at the threshold of the higher level.

7.2 Coverage

  • Literacy: 100%
  • Secondary education: 98%
  • Higher/guild education: 72%
  • Academic degree: 12%

Coverage indicators result from two factors: free and accessible education (→ rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 03, "Khal-Ven Rass"]) and a cultural norm fixed in the canon.

7.3 Guild Training

Higher education organized through the guilds (→ rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 04, "Ruun-El Xha-Khal"]):

  • Apprenticeship — practice under a master
  • Trials — the stages "apprentice → journeyman → master"
  • Academies — research centers and universities

Guild training does not end with the attainment of a rank: lifelong professional development is the norm, ensuring knowledge remains current amid technological progress.

7.4 Academies

Academies — the peak of the educational pyramid: research institutions united with universities. The largest — the Academy of Khar-Vexar (+1 200, → rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 10, "The-Ven Thar-El"]). Academies train scientific cadres and conduct fundamental research.

7.5 Education and Mobility

Education = the main channel of social mobility (→ rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 06, "Xha-Mor The-Rass"]). The accessibility of education neutralizes the influence of origin: any child, regardless of family, can travel the path to an academic degree or guild mastery.
