Xha'Ruun-Vokh: Sha-En Ven-El (The First Word)

A story in the Xha'Ruun language. Time: −9 780 Y.B.

El the-vex the-ven en-ak-ven. — She was sitting at the clay wall of the hut. (She earth-on earth-inessive be-PAST-DIR)

El khal-thar sha-ven en-ak-ven. — She was looking at her fingers. (She finger-ACC see be-PAST-DIR)

Khal-mor the-vex thar-el the-ven — Fingers leaving marks on wet clay. (Finger-from earth-on mark-PL earth-inessive — conceptual)

Translation Notes:

  • All sentences: SOV order
  • Verbs: mandatory evidentiality suffix
  • Past tense: -ak
  • Direct evidence: -ven (the narrator sees)
  • Plural: -el on thar (mark)
  • Cases: the-vex = on earth (superessive -vex), the-ven = to/in earth (dative -ven)

End of story in Xha'Ruun.
