Thexar-VII: The-Xa — Sha-El 05: Vex-Khal Xa-Sha — Cave Ecosystems

"Below, beneath the Central Plain, there is a world where there is no day or night. Where rivers flow in total darkness for millions of cycles. And where life has forgotten what light looks like." — Speleologist Khal-Ven, +3,500 y.

05.2 Cave Energetics

In the absence of light, primary production is provided by H₂S and Fe²⁺ chemosynthesis.

Ruun Ven

  1. Théxar's karst vex-khal form the planet's largest subterranean ecosystems, extending beneath the Central Plain.
  2. Total explored passage length reaches ~340,000 мо, covering ~0.8 million мо² of underground habitat.
  3. Thal-Ven is the deepest known cave system, descending 2,840 мо below the surface.
  4. Cave endemism is exceptionally high at ~95%, reflecting long isolation from surface biotas.
  5. Without sunlight, primary production is driven entirely by H₂S and Fe²⁺ chemosynthesis.
  6. Speleologist Khal-Ven (+3,500 y.) framed these caves as a lightless world where rivers have flowed in darkness for millions of cycles.
