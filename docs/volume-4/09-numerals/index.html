Volume IV: Language — Chapter 9: Senary Numeral System

"Six is the foundation of everything. 6 limbs, 6 letters of the genetic code, 6 digits in our system. Six is the number of harmony." — Mathematical Treatise, +2 000 Y.E.

Related sections: → see [Volume IV, Ch.03, "Grammar: Overview"], → see [Volume I, Ch.34, "Measurement Systems of Théxar"] Length: 15 pages

Introduction

The language and mathematics of the Xha'Ruun are based on the senary numeral system. The choice of base 6 is motivated by biology (six limbs) and genetics (a six-letter code). The senary system is not only a way of writing numbers but also a cultural constant, reflected in the calendar, in measurements, and in architecture.

9.1 The Digits

Digit Symbol (Unicode) Name
0 U+E000 mor (void)
1 U+E001 ven (one)
2 U+E002 sha (two)
3 U+E003 tor (three)
4 U+E004 vex (four)
5 U+E005 rass (five)

The names of the digits coincide with the basic roots (→ see [Volume IV, Ch.08, "Lexicon and Word Formation"]): ven (truth), sha (language), tor (strength), vex (home), rass (accord) — numbers are conceptually connected with cultural meanings.

9.2 Examples of Counting

Decimal Senary Xha'Ruun
0 0 mor
1 1 ven
6 10 ven-mor
12 20 sha-mor
36 100 tor-mor-mor
216 1000 vex-mor-mor-mor

The place value is senary: "ven-mor" = 1×6 + 0 = 6. Compound numerals are agglutinative — each place is attached with the suffix -mor.

9.3 Senary Mathematics

The senary system defines the mathematical culture:

  • Fractions — positional, in powers of 6;
  • Calendar — a year of 412.7 days, 14 months (→ see [Volume II, Ch.17, "History of the Calendar"]);
  • Measurements — units of length, mass, and time are multiples of 6 (→ see [Volume I, Ch.34, "Measurement Systems of Théxar"]).

9.4 Cultural Significance

The number 6 is a cultural constant: 6 limbs, 6 letters of the code, 6 digits, 6 spatial cases, 6 laws of the canon. Senary harmony pervades language, science, and religion.
