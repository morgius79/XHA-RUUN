Volume IV: Language — Chapter 5: Verbs and Tense

"The verb is the engine of the sentence. It carries action, tense, and the source of information. Without a verb, there is no utterance." — Grammar of Xha'Ruun, Academy

Related sections: → see [Volume IV, Ch.03, "Grammar: Overview"], → see [Volume IV, Ch.06, "Evidentiality"] Length: 25 pages

Introduction

The Xha'Ruun verbal system is agglutinative, with rich inflectional morphology. The verb carries action, tense, and — necessarily — the source of information (evidentiality). This chapter describes conjugation, the tense system, and verbal word formation.

5.1 Conjugation

Xha'Ruun verbs conjugate for:

  • Tense (4 types);
  • Number (3: singular, dual, plural);
  • Evidentiality (4 types — mandatory).

The subject does not agree in person — person is optionally expressed by pronominal clitics. Evidentiality is mandatory: the speaker is obliged to indicate the source of information (→ see [Volume IV, Ch.06]).

5.2 Tense System

Tense Suffix Example Translation
Past -ak khal-ak did
Present -en khal-en does
Future -mor khal-mor will do
Gnomic -el khal-el does (always)

The gnomic tense (-el) marks truths outside of time: laws, generalizations, canon. This form is widely used in sacred texts and scientific formulations.

5.3 Suffix Order

The verbal form is built according to the scheme:

Stem + tense + number + evidentiality

Example: khal-en-ak-ven = "does (plural, I see)". The suffix order is fixed and mandatory.

5.4 Aspect and Voice

Besides tense, the verb can express:

  • Aspect — perfective/imperfective (the suffix -thal for resultativity);
  • Voice — active/passive (passive -vex-thar);
  • Causative — the suffix -khal-ven.

These categories are optional and attach between the stem and the tense suffix.

5.5 Example

Xha'ruun-sha the-thar khal-en-ven
People-ERG soil-ACC do-NPST-DIR
"The people cultivate the soil (I see it)"

Chapter Summary

  1. Conjugation for tense (4), number (3), evidentiality (4 — mandatory).
  2. The gnomic tense (-el) — for truths outside of time.
  3. Suffix order: stem + tense + number + evidentiality.
  4. Aspect, voice, causative — optional categories.
  5. Evidentiality is mandatory in every verbal form.
