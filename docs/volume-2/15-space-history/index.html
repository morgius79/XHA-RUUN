Volume II: History — Chapter 15: History of the Space Era

"The first step into space — a step into immortality. Because as long as we fly — we live." — Thash-Ven, first cosmonaut of Théxar, +480 Y.E.

Related sections: → see [Volume I, Ch.09, "Technology"], → see [Volume I, Ch.28, "History of Exploration"], → see [Volume I, Ch.32, "The Khar'Vex System"] Length: 25 pages

Introduction

The space era began for Xha'Ruun civilization in +480 — with the first orbital flight — and over three and a half millennia led to the creation of full-scale space infrastructure: orbital stations, satellite systems, exploration of the moons, and the first interstellar probe. This chapter traces the chronology of this path and its significance.

15.1 Early Cosmonautics

Year Achievement Ship / Mission
+480 First orbital flight "Khar-Veks-1" (pilot Thash-Ven)
+520 First satellite "Ser-Mor"
+600 First flight to Serrin "Ven-Khal" (unmanned)
+1,200 First landing on Serrin "Khal-Ven" (manned)
+2,100 First landing on Thel "Tel"
+3,300 Permanent base on Thel "He-3-1"

The first orbital flight (+480, the ship "Khar-Veks-1") was the result of six decades of rocket programs begun at the Academy of Khar-Vexar. Pilot Thash-Ven entered history as the first Xha'ri to leave the limits of the home planet (→ see [Volume I, Ch.28, "History of Exploration"]).

15.2 Development of Infrastructure

Period Infrastructure
+800 – +1,200 First orbital stations
+2,800 GNSS satellite system (6 → 24)
+3,000 Orbital elevator (1)
+4,000 48 satellites, 24 stations

The key milestone — the orbital elevator (+3,000, "Khar-Tor"): it radically reduced the cost of delivering cargo to orbit and made space infrastructure economically viable (→ see [Volume I, Ch.09, "Technology"]). A second elevator is under construction (completion ~+4,080).

15.3 Exploration of the Moons

15.3.1 Serrin

The first landing on Serrin (+1,200) — the most important stage of near-space exploration. Serrin, the largest moon of Théxar, became a base for research programs and, in the future, resource extraction.

15.3.2 Thel

Thel — a C-type asteroid rich in helium-3. The first landing (+2,100), permanent base (+3,300, "He-3-1"), and automation of extraction made Thel a planetary-scale energy resource: most of the He-3 for fusion energy comes from its surface (→ see [Volume I, Ch.33, "Fusion and Stellar Energy"]).

15.4 Beyond the System

In +3,800 an interstellar probe was launched toward the nearest star (5.2 light-years). As of +4,028 it continues its flight — the first artificial object to leave the limits of the Khar'Vex system (→ see [Volume II, Ch.07, "The Modern Era"]).

The projects to expand the Xhel base (+4,200) and the second elevator (~+4,080) indicate the direction of the next stage — turning the Khar'Vex system into a unified industrial zone.

Chapter Summary

  1. The space era began with the first orbital flight (+480, "Khar-Veks-1").
  2. Landings: Serrin (+1,200), Thel (+2,100); the base on Thel (+3,300).
  3. Infrastructure: stations (+800 – +1,200), GNSS (+2,800), elevator (+3,000), 48 satellites and 24 stations (+4,000).
  4. The interstellar probe was launched in +3,800 toward the nearest star.
  5. The next stage: the second elevator and expansion of the Xhel base.
