## Introduction

Théxar is the only known planet to have given rise to not one but three independently arisen sentient species. This chapter closes the naturalistic survey of Volume I, drawing together what chapters 5, 38, and 39 described separately: how three radically different life forms share a single planet — not by displacing one another, but by complementing each other, occupying three distinct niches of one world. → see [Ch.37, epigraph: "Théxar is a planet without empty niches"]

## 40.2 The Council of Unity and the Principle of Three

> **Canon §4.3: Law of Accord**
>
> No decision of the Council of Unity may be made without consulting representatives of all three sentient species of Théxar — Xha'Ruun, Kel'vash, and Moryn. This provision, known as the **Principle of Three**, was established upon the Kel'vash admission to the Council (+1,200 yr.) and confirmed upon the Moryn admission (+1,500 yr.).

The Principle of Three is not merely a diplomatic formality but a structural response to a problem Xha'Ruun civilization recognized early: the three species perceive the world, time, and ethics so differently that decisions made by only one of them systematically undervalue the interests of the other two.

### 40.2.1 The Reconciliation Mechanism

Because the three species communicate, think, and act at radically different tempos (seconds to minutes for Xha'Ruun, hours to days for Kel'vash, a dialogue slowed roughly threefold for Moryn), the Council of Unity developed a multi-stage protocol:

1. **Initiation** — the matter is raised in Xha'Ruun spoken language (the Council's working language)
2. **Translation** — specialized devices ("tempo translators" for Kel'vash, transluminators for Moryn) convert the matter into vibrational and bioluminescent formats
3. **Deliberation** — each species considers the matter at its own pace (from hours to several days for complex decisions)
4. **Reconciliation** — the responses are synchronized and presented simultaneously
5. **Ratification** — the decision takes effect only if none of the three species objects

## 40.4 Interspecies Communication Barriers

> **TABLE 40.3: Communication Barriers**
>
> | Barrier | Xha'Ruun ↔ Kel'vash | Xha'Ruun ↔ Moryn | Kel'vash ↔ Moryn |
> |---------|---------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
> | Tempo | ~40× difference | ~3× difference | ~13× difference |
> | Channel | Sound ↔ vibration | Sound ↔ light | Vibration ↔ light |
> | Privacy of thought | Compatible | Incompatible (Moryn thought is public) | Both "transparent" to differing degrees |
> | Temporal ethics | Kel'vash — "geological" thinking | Moryn — collective present | Requires double translation |

The most difficult remains direct dialogue between Kel'vash and Moryn — two species whose communication has never naturally overlapped (mountains and the deep ocean do not touch). All contact between them passes through Xha'Ruun mediation and a double technical conversion of the signal.

## 40.5 Ecological Unity of Théxar

The three species occupy three mutually exclusive ecological niches, together covering nearly the full range of conditions suitable for complex life on Théxar:

```
        THE THREE NICHES OF SENTIENT LIFE ON THÉXAR

  Altitude/Depth           Species          Temperature
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
   +2,400…+9,500 sha     Kel'vash          180–220°X
        (mountains/deserts)
   ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
      0…+2,400 sha       Xha'Ruun          ~30–65°X
        (all land)
   ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
   −1,200…−3,000 sha       Moryn             2–8°X
        (Gharra depths)
```

*Fig. 40.1. The three niches of sentient life, by vertical profile of the planet.*

None of the three species competes directly with the others for territory or resources — which, in the view of Xha'Ruun historians, is one reason first contact (unlike hypothetical scenarios in Xha'Ruun fiction, → see [Ch.10, "Mythology"]) led not to conflict but to cooperation.
