## Introduction

The chapter on the present is the only chapter of Volume II that describes not the past but the current state of the civilization. In +4,028 E.U. the Xha'Ruun stand on the threshold of a qualitative transition: from a planetary civilization to an interstellar one. Technologies of abundance, a stable political system, and a growing space infrastructure pose problems that no previous era knew.

---

## 7.1 The Current State

By 4,028 E.U. the Xha'Ruun civilization has achieved:

| Parameter | Value | Status |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Population | 2.8 billion (Xha'ri) · all species ≈ 4.8 billion | Stable growth +0.3%/year |
| Urbanization | 62% | Stable |
| Energy | ~2,308 GW (62% fusion) | Post-scarcity |
| Economy | 124 trillion s.u. | Growth +2.8%/year |
| Science | 7.2% of GDP on R&D | Growing |
| Space | 48 satellites, 24 stations | Developing |

The key economic indicator — **post-scarcity**: basic resources (energy, food, housing) are available to every inhabitant of the planet regardless of status. The economic problem has shifted from scarcity to distribution and meaning (→ see [Volume II, Ch.09, "Economic History"]).

## 7.2 Modern Challenges

### 7.2.1 The Demographic Challenge

Population growth is consciously regulated (the **Growth Commandment**, no more than 0.3% per year). The mechanism is not coercion but cultural norm and economic planning: every household participates in resource distribution in accordance with the commandment. The goal is to avoid repeating the demographic crisis of the First Empire (→ see [Volume II, Ch.16, "Demographics"]).

### 7.2.2 The Resource Challenge

| Resource | Status | Measure |
|----------|--------|---------|
| He-3 | Rising extraction costs | Development of new technologies |
| Rare-earth elements | Sufficient | 95% recycling |
| Thorium | Surplus | More than 50,000 years |

The main resource limit is **helium-3**, extracted on the moon Thel (→ see [Volume I, Ch.33, "Fusion and Stellar Energy"]). Reserves are large (~60.6 million kh), but growing consumption stimulates the search for alternatives and more efficient extraction.

### 7.2.3 The Cultural Challenge

Preserving cultural diversity under a single civilization: 12 official languages, 8 domains, 12 guilds, three sapient species. The Council's policy — "unity without uniformity": common norms with maximum autonomy of cultural traditions.

---

## 7.3 Planetary Defense

A system for detecting and deflecting dangerous asteroids:

- 8 telescopes (optical + IR);
- 12 interceptors (kinetic + nuclear);
- Early warning system: 30 days.

Defense is the only sphere that retained a military component after the abolition of interregional conflicts. Its task is not protection from an enemy but protection from natural threats: the planet's history knows cases of significant impact events (→ see [Volume I, Ch.12, "Geological Evolution"]).

## 7.5 Looking Ahead

The present is a moment when history ceases to be only the past. Three long-term questions define the coming centuries:

1. **The path to the stars** — can a civilization built on one planet carry itself to other worlds?
2. **Preservation of unity** — will the Council system withstand the pressure of interstellar expansion?
3. **The meaning of abundance** — what does a society do in which all material needs are satisfied?

The answers to these questions will likely be determined by the next era of Xha'Ruun history.

2. Demographic growth is regulated by the Growth Commandment (≤0.3%/year).
3. The main resource limit is helium-3; thorium is abundant.
4. Planetary defense is the only remaining military structure.
5. Key projects: second elevator, Xhel base, interstellar probe (in transit since +3,800).
6. Three strategic questions: expansion, unity, the meaning of abundance.
