## Introduction

The Era of Unity is the longest and most stable era in the history of Xha'Ruun civilization. Three-plus millennia of continuous peace, technological progress, and social development without a single war between states. This stability is not chance and not the result of suppression, but the consequence of an institutional choice made at the founding of the Council of Unity.

The era began with political formalization (+850) and passed through four technological phases — industrial, thermonuclear, informational, and the era of abundance. To the present moment (+4,028) the civilization has achieved a post-scarcity economy, full literacy, and readiness to move beyond its home planet.

## 6.1 The Founding of the Council of Unity (+850)

**+850 y.c.** (EV-0011) — 49 representatives gathered in Khar-Vexar to create the supreme authority of the civilization. The first chair was Ven-Thal-Vek, a physicist and diplomat.

### 6.1.1 Structure of the Council

The Council of Unity (Rass-Vokh-Sha) is built on the principle of dual representation:

| Component | Composition | Function |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Guild Chamber | 24 members (2 from each of the 12 guilds) | Economy, science, technology |
| Region Chamber | 25 members (from the eight domains) | Territory, population, culture |
| Keeper of Knowledge | 1 (lifelong, without vote) | Control of canon compliance |

Total **49 members** (24 + 25). The distribution between guilds and regions fixes the balance of the two forces that shaped the civilization: professional associations (guilds) and territorial communities (domains). The Keeper of Knowledge performs a constitutional function — ensuring that Council decisions do not contradict the canon.

### 6.1.2 First Decisions

1. Confirmation of the Unified Calendar (introduced in 0);
2. Official recognition of the guild system (12 guilds);
3. Creation of a unified writing system based on classical Sha'Ruun;
4. Establishment of the Keeper Guild as guardian of the canon and archives.

The construction of the orbital elevator — a much later event (+3,000); in the Council's first decades the agenda was set by standardization and institutional building.

## 6.4 Technological Progress by Era

### The Industrial Era (−1,200 – +1,420)

Steam, coal, steel; railroad construction; electrification. Although its beginning lies in the previous era, it was in the first centuries of the Era of Unity that industrialization covered the whole planet.

### The Thermonuclear Era (+1,420 – +2,000)

Controlled fusion, the end of fossil fuels, the first orbital stations. Energy shifts to thorium and He-3 (→ see [Volume I, Ch.33, "Fusion and Stellar Energy"]).

### The Informational Era (+1,800 – +2,800)

Bio-optical computing, quantum communication; limited artificial intelligence. The first planetary-scale computing networks were created.

### The Era of Abundance (+2,800 – present)

Post-scarcity economy, full automation, exploration of the moons (the base on Thel). Energy and basic resources are available to everyone; the economic problem shifts from scarcity to distribution.

|-----------|------|--------|
| Population (Xha'ri) | ~0.4 billion | ~2.8 billion |
| Urbanization | 18% | 62% |
| Literacy | 40% | 100% |
| Average lifespan | ~80 years | ~180 years |
| GDP per capita | — | 44,000 s.u. |
| Slavery | Existed | Abolished (+1,500) |

Population growth is restrained by the **Growth Commandment** (no more than 0.3% per year), adopted in the aftermath of the First Empire crisis. All the civilization's species — Xha'ri (~2.8 billion), Kel'vash (~1.2 billion), and Moryn (~0.8 billion) — total ~4.8 billion (→ see [Volume II, Ch.14, "The Three Species"] and [Volume II, Ch.16, "Demographics"]).
