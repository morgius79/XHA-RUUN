## Introduction

A domain is the basic territorial unit of modern Xha'Ruun civilization. The eight domains cover all continents of Théxar and represent not merely administrative districts but historical-cultural regions, each with its own identity, economic specialization, and history. The political structure rests on the principle of "unity without uniformity": common Council norms with maximum autonomy of the domains.

## 11.2 History of Formation

The boundaries of the domains reflect historical and cultural differences formed over millennia:

- **Kharak-Thar** — the oldest domain, the core of the First Empire and the site of Khar-Vexar's founding (−7,800); the "heart" of the civilization;
- **Kharak-Tor** — the agrarian domain, separated from central Kharak (+120);
- **Rhuval-Ven** — the trade center of Rhuval, heir of the Serrin region; joined the Unity Treaty (−750);
- **Rhuval-Irkhen** — the industrial domain around the city of Irkhen (+200);
- **Vexar-Shan** — the technological domain, center of the Engineers and Artists guilds; joined Unity last (+850);
- **Vexar-Mor** — the energy domain of eastern Vexar (+1,200);
- **Island** — the Khal-Tor archipelago, the oldest cultural center (settled since −5,000);
- **Polar** — scientific stations of the polar regions (+2,500), without a traditional permanent population.

## 11.3 Domains as Cultural Regions

Each domain possesses a distinct identity:

| Domain | Identity | Symbol |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Kharak-Thar | Administrative core | Golden star |
| Kharak-Tor | Agrarian | Ear of grain |
| Rhuval-Ven | Trade | Ship |
| Rhuval-Irkhen | Industrial | Hammer |
| Vexar-Shan | Technological | Gear |
| Vexar-Mor | Energy | Flame |
| Island | Cultural | Wave |
| Polar | Scientific | Ice star |

These identities are fixed in coats of arms, dialects, and cultural traditions. Preserving diversity is a conscious policy of the Council of Unity: the domains delegate to it **25 of 49** members.

---

## 11.4 Domains and Guilds

The political system balances two cross-sections of society: the **domains** (territorial) and the **guilds** (professional). Each guild has its headquarters in a specific domain (for example, the Miners Guild in Rhuval-Irkhen, the Artists Guild in Vexar-Shan), linking the economic and territorial structures (→ see [Volume II, Ch.08, "History of the Guilds"]).
