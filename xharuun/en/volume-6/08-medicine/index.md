## Introduction

Xha'Ruun medicine is unique in the known world not so much for its technologies as for the scale of its biological problem: it must serve three sentient species with fundamentally different biochemistries. The chambered carbon life of the Xha'Ruun (→ see [Volume I, Ch.05]), the silicon-organic Kel'vash (→ see [Volume I, Ch.38]), and the deep-water biophotonics of the Moryn (→ see [Volume I, Ch.39]) require not merely different protocols but different models of health and disease. The unified Guild of Medics (→ see [Volume III, Ch.04]) unites all three traditions within common ethical principles, while each species preserves its own methodology.

## 8.1 Three Biological Paradigms

| Parameter | Xha'Ruun | Kel'vash | Moryn |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------|
| Base polymer | Carbon | Silicon | Carbon + photonics |
| Working solvent | H₂O + NH₃ | H₂O + silicates | H₂O + salts |
| Metabolism | Aerobic photosynthesis + chemosynthesis | Chemoautotrophy (Si + S) | Bioluminescent chemosynthesis |
| Neural system | Bioelectrical | Vibrational (mechanical) | Photonic (biophotonics) |
| Neural signal frequency | 0.1–100 kHz | 0.001–0.6 Hz | 10¹⁴–10¹⁵ Hz (optical) |
| Thermoregulation | Endothermic + heliothermy | Passive (conduction) | Thermal stability of the environment |
| Standard body temperature | 41.2 °C | −2 °C – +180 °C (variable) | 2.1 °C |
| Life expectancy | 150–200 local years | 1,200–3,000 local years | 400–800 local years |
| Reproduction | Egg-laying (1 clutch / 3–4 years) | Binary division (once every 60–120 years) | Spore colonization (annually) |

A key terminological point: the concept of "health" differs for each species. For the Xha'Ruun it is the maintenance of homeostasis within endothermic physiology. For the Kel'vash it is the maintenance of the crystalline integrity of the skeleton and the continuity of the vibrational channels. For the Moryn it is the continuity of the bioluminescent pattern that reflects the cognitive state.

## 8.2 Medical Technologies

### 8.2.1 Molecular Surgery (Xha'Ruun)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Precision | 1 molecule (10⁻¹⁰ m) |
| Instrument | Bio-manipulators (living, nerve-controlled) |
| Intervention | Insertion/removal of a single nucleotide in the genome |
| Operation time | 2–12 hours |
| Side effects | Rare (< 0.1%) |
| First application | +2,800 Y.E. |

Nerve-controlled bio-manipulators are living organisms of class 5 (→ see [Volume VI, Ch.07, "Materials Science"]), directed by the surgeon through the Xha-Ven interface. They enter the patient's body through natural openings or micro-incisions and work at the cellular level.

### 8.2.2 Regenerative Medicine

Xha'Ruun tissue regeneration is based on enhancing natural restorative abilities (4 lungs, a paired cardiac system, skin capillaries — → see [Volume I, Ch.05, §6.2]).

| Structure | Full regeneration time | Method |
|-----------|------------------------|--------|
| Skin (up to 1 dm²) | 4 days | Natural |
| Skin (full replacement) | 60 days | Accelerating stimuli |
| Muscle tissue | 24–48 days | Bio-stimulation |
| Nervous tissue | 180–360 days | Molecular surgery |
| Limbs | 2–4 years | Combined method |
| Organs (internal) | 1–3 years | Bio-printer + regeneration |

**The bio-printer** — a device that prints a three-dimensional scaffold from silicon-organic material, on which the patient's own tissue is grown. The scaffold degrades as the tissue restores and is fully absorbed.

### 8.2.3 Gene Therapy

Xha'Ruun gene therapy is based on the six-letter genetic code (6 nucleotides: G, C, A, T, P, X — → see [Volume I, Ch.04, §5.1]), which gives greater correction flexibility but also greater complexity:

| Protocol | Description | Application |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Single-nucleotide mutation correction | Replacement of one of the 6 nucleotides | Monogenic diseases |
| Multiple correction | Simultaneous replacement of up to 6 positions | Polygenic predispositions |
| Epigenetic rearrangement | Methylation/demethylation | Stress, aging |
| Inter-species transfer | Insertion of a gene from another species | Experimental (Kel'vash genes for heat resistance) |

### 8.2.4 Cryogenics and Anabiosis

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Maximum anabiosis | 200 local years |
| Storage temperature | −196 °C (liquid nitrogen) |
| Recovery | 12–36 hours |
| Neural preservation | 99.97% |
| First application | +2,900 Y.E. |
| Current application | Interstellar probes, medical anabiosis |

### 8.2.5 Diagnostics

Xha'Ruun diagnostics uses three levels:

| Level | Method | What It Determines |
|-------|--------|--------------------|
| Basic | Bio-sensors on the skin | Hormones, temperature, vibrations |
| Advanced | Intracellular nano-sensors | Cell state in real time |
| Research | The planetary Xha-Ven network | Epidemiological patterns |

Nano-sensors (class 5, → see [Volume VI, Ch.07]) are living organisms 5–50 μm in size, introduced into the bloodstream. They swim, tracking the chemical composition of the blood, and transmit data through Xha-Ven to a diagnostic center.

## 8.3 Kel'vash Medicine

The Kel'vash do not fall ill in the biological sense of the Xha'Ruun, but they are subject to degradation of the crystalline body:

| Problem | Description | Solution |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Skeleton cracks | Mechanical damage | Healing with a silicon "patch" |
| Surface corrosion | Interaction with oxides | Re-mineralization |
| Vibration desynchronization | Disruption of the "neural" channel | Resonance therapy |
| Core degradation | Aging of the central structure | Irreversible; ~3,000 years |
| Thermal shock | Sudden temperature change | Slow adaptation |

**Uniqueness:** the Kel'vash have no immune system in the carbon sense. Their "defense" is crystalline integrity; for them an infection is any substance that disrupts the lattice.

## 8.4 Moryn Medicine

The Moryn inhabit depths down to 9,847 m (the Thash Trench — → see [Volume I, Ch.03, §4.4]), where pressure reaches ~1,000 atm. Moryn medicine is specialized for:

| Problem | Description | Solution |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Barotrauma | Pressure above/below the optimum | Acoustic compensation |
| Luminescence pattern disruption | Cognitive failure | "Light diet" — correction of the photon flux |
| Colonial fragmentation | Loss of part of the colony | Spore reintegration |
| Acid corrosion | Aggressive deep-water environment | Bio-incrustation |

**Uniqueness:** the Moryn do not distinguish physical and mental health — their "illness" is always simultaneously physical and cognitive, since body and mind are connected by the bioluminescent pattern.

## 8.5 Inter-Species Medicine

The inter-species medicine program was created in +1,600 Y.E. — 400 years after the Kel'vash entered the Council of Unity and 100 years after the Moryn.

| Program | Year | Description |
|---------|------|-------------|
| Common diagnostic standards | +1,600 | Unified disease classification |
| Transplants between species | +2,400 | Experimental (limited) |
| Common emergency care protocol | +2,200 | Adapted to all three body types |
| Psychiatric care | +2,800 | Accounting for differences in neural systems |

**Limits:** organ transplantation between species is practically impossible because of biochemical differences. The exception — Kel'vash silicon-organic structures, which can be used as **scaffolds** for the regeneration of Xha'Ruun tissue (the method is described in §8.2.2: the "bio-printer" uses class-6 scaffold materials developed jointly with the Kel'vash).

## 8.6 Ethical Restrictions

| Prohibition | Reason | Year Introduced |
|-------------|--------|-----------------|
| Cloning of a person | Violation of individual integrity | +1,400 |
| Immortality | Disruption of evolutionary cycles | +2,400 |
| Memory rewriting | Violation of identity | +2,400 |
| Forced treatment | The right to refuse (Guild Code) | +1,200 |
| Inter-species genetic exchange (except scaffolds) | Unpredictability of mutations | +2,400 |

**Philosophical foundation:** Xha'Ruun medicine does not strive for "eternal youth" or the complete abolition of death. Health is the ability to realize one's life to the full, not its endless prolongation (→ see [Volume V, Ch.05, "Philosophy"]). The life limit of the Kel'vash (~3,000 years) and the Moryn (~800 years) is respected as part of their nature, not as a problem to be solved.

## Chapter Summary

1. Xha'Ruun medicine serves three sentient species with fundamentally different biochemistries: carbon (Xha'Ruun), silicon-organic (Kel'vash), and biophotonic (Moryn).
2. The key technologies are molecular surgery with single-molecule precision, tissue regeneration through bio-printers, and gene therapy on the six-letter code.
3. Gene therapy relies on 6 nucleotides instead of 4, giving greater correction flexibility at the cost of higher complexity.
4. Ethics bounds medicine: immortality, cloning of a person, and forced treatment are forbidden; death is regarded as part of the natural cycle.
5. Anabiosis and cryogenics technologies open a span of up to 200 local years and are used chiefly in interstellar programs.
