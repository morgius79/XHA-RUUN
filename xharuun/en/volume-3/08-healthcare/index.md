## Introduction

Xha'Ruun healthcare is a universal system ensuring the right to health for every representative of all three species. A reflection of society's values: medicine is organized through the Medic Guild but funded from the basic goods of the post-scarcity economy.

---

## 8.1 The System

- **Free for all citizens** — basic medical provision;
- **1.2 million medics** (including Kel'vash and Moryn specialists);
- **Average lifespan: 180 local years** (Xha'ri);
- **Infant mortality: 2.1 per 1,000**.

The system covers all three species: Kel'vash medicine (silicon-organic biochemistry) and Moryn medicine (oceanic physiology) are specialized directions within the Medic Guild.

---

## 8.2 Medical Achievements

| Achievement | Year | Result |
|-------------|------|--------|
| Khal-Ven antibiotic | +1,400 | Infections defeated |
| Tissue regeneration | +2,450 | Organ restoration |
| Gene therapy | +2,800 | Hereditary diseases eliminated |
| Lifespan extension | +3,200 | +80 years to the average |
| Full rejuvenation | +3,800 | Theoretically possible |

The key principle of medical ethics — **biological caution** (→ see [Volume II, Ch.10, "History of Science"]): the legacy of the "Red Rot" pandemic (−2,120) restricts risky research. Gene therapy and lifespan extension were introduced gradually, with collegial review.

---

## 8.3 Prevention and Public Health

The post-scarcity society emphasizes prevention:

- **Sanitary norms** — the historical legacy of epidemiological experience;
- **Public nutrition** — food quality control;
- **Screening** — early detection of hereditary risks;
- **Mental health** — support through the guilds.

---

## 8.4 Ethics and Limits

Xha'Ruun medicine operates within ethical frameworks:

- **Right of refusal** — no intervention is forced;
- **Three species** — protocols account for all species' biology;
- **Rejuvenation** — available but voluntary; a public discussion of its social consequences continues.
