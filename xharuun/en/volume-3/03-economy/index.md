## Introduction

The economic system of modern Xha'Ruun civilization is the result of a centuries-long transition from an economy of scarcity to an economy of abundance. Its key feature is **post-scarcity**: basic resources ceased to be limited, and the economic problem shifted from survival to distribution and meaning.

---

## 3.1 The Post-Scarcity Economy

The Xha'Ruun economy is post-scarcity. Basic resources are available to everyone without payment.

| Resource | Status | Provision |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Energy | Free | Fusion + Khar'Vex |
| Food | Free (basic) | Automated agriculture |
| Housing | Free (basic) | Autonomous blocks |
| Medicine | Free | Medic Guild |
| Education | Free | Scholars Guild |

Post-scarcity does not mean the abolition of economics: the market survives for **differentiated goods** — handcraft, art, unique services. Value shifts from material possession to quality, uniqueness, and meaning.

---

## 3.2 The Guild Economy

72% of GDP is controlled by the 12 Guilds. The remaining 28% — independent producers and services.

| Indicator | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Planetary GDP | 124 trillion s.u. |
| GDP per capita | 44,000 s.u. |
| Guild share of GDP | 72% |
| Growth | 2.8%/year |
| Unemployment | 2.8% |

The guilds are simultaneously economic actors and regulators: they own key industries, set standards, and organize training (→ see [Volume II, Ch.08, "History of the Guilds"]).

---

## 3.4 Distribution

The distribution system is basic (by need) + market (by preference):

1. **Basic distribution** — energy, food, housing, medicine, education — by need, without payment;
2. **Market sector** — differentiated goods and services — through guild exchanges and independent producers;
3. **Social guarantees** — pensions, support in work and old age — through the guilds.

---

## 3.5 Labor and Motivation

In the post-scarcity economy, labor is **voluntary**. Motivation shifts from survival to:

- **Professional fulfillment** — guild career;
- **Public recognition** — contribution to science, culture, infrastructure;
- **Personal meaning** — creativity, research, service.

Unemployment (2.8%) is not an economic problem but a period of retraining or a conscious choice of profession.

## Chapter Summary

1. The economy is post-scarcity: basic resources are free.

3. Energy (2,308 GW, 62% fusion) — the foundation of abundance.

5. Labor is voluntary; motivation — fulfillment, recognition, meaning.
