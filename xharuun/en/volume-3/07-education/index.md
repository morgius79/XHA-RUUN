## Introduction

Education is the foundation of Xha'Ruun civilization, its highest institutional priority. The lesson of the dark ages (→ see [Volume II, Ch.04, "The Era of Crisis"]) — knowledge is fragile, and its transmission requires a system. The modern education system is continuous: from primary school to lifelong guild training.

---

## 7.1 The Education System

| Level | Age | Duration | Content |
|-------|-----|----------|---------|
| Primary | 5–10 years | 5 years | Writing, arithmetic, canon |
| Secondary | 10–15 years | 5 years | Sciences, languages, guilds |
| Higher (Guild) | 15–20 years | 5 years | Specialization |
| Academy | 20+ | 3–8 years | Research work |

The system is built on the principle of **progressive narrowing**: from universal primary education to specialized guild training. Secondary education introduces students to all 12 guilds' activities; the choice of guild occurs at the threshold of the higher level.

---

## 7.2 Coverage

- Literacy: **100%**
- Secondary education: **98%**
- Higher/guild education: **72%**
- Academic degree: **12%**

Coverage indicators result from two factors: free and accessible education (→ see [Volume III, Ch.03, "Economic System"]) and a cultural norm fixed in the canon.

---

## 7.3 Guild Training

Higher education is organized through the guilds (→ see [Volume III, Ch.04, "The 12 Guilds: Structure"]):

- **Apprenticeship** — practice under a master;
- **Trials** — the stages "apprentice → journeyman → master";
- **Academies** — research centers and universities.

Guild training does not end with the attainment of a rank: lifelong professional development is the norm, ensuring knowledge remains current amid technological progress.

---

## 7.4 Academies

**Academies** — the peak of the educational pyramid: research institutions united with universities. The largest — the Academy of Khar-Vexar (+1,200, → see [Volume II, Ch.10, "History of Science"]). Academies train scientific cadres and conduct fundamental research.

---

## 7.5 Education and Mobility

Education is the main channel of social mobility (→ see [Volume III, Ch.06, "Social Structure"]). The accessibility of education neutralizes the influence of origin: any child, regardless of family, can travel the path to an academic degree or guild mastery.

## Chapter Summary

1. Education — a continuous system: primary → secondary → guild → academy.
2. Coverage: literacy 100%, secondary 98%, guild 72%, academies 12%.
3. Guild training — apprenticeship and lifelong development.
4. Academies — the peak of the educational and scientific pyramid.
5. Education — the main channel of social mobility.
