## Introduction

Science is the institutional foundation of Xha'Ruun civilization. Its organization is governed by two principles: **openness** (knowledge belongs to all) and **guild coordination** (research is managed by the Scholars Guild). The organization of science spans institutes, universities, observatories, and laboratories.

---

## 10.1 Structure

| Element | Count | Function |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Institutes | 240 | Research |
| Universities | 48 | Education |
| Observatories | 36 | Astronomy |
| Laboratories | 8,200 | Applied science |

The network of institutes and laboratories is distributed across all domains (→ see [Volume III, Ch.05, "The 8 Domains"]), ensuring a diversity of scientific schools. The astronomical network includes orbital observatories and stations on the moons.

---

## 10.2 Budget

- R&D spending: **7.2% of GDP**;
- Scientists: **8.4 million** (all species);
- Priorities: energy, biotechnology, space.

The level of 7.2% of GDP on R&D — among the highest of known civilizations. Funding does not depend on market conditions: fundamental research is supported as a basic value.

---

## 10.3 Guild Coordination

Science is organized through the **Scholars Guild** (→ see [Volume III, Ch.04, "The 12 Guilds: Structure"]):

- **Program coordination** — priorities and resource allocation;
- **Publication** — a unified open access system;
- **Collegial review** — review of the most important discoveries;
- **Industry links** — transfer of results to engineering, medicine, industry.

The role of the **Keeper Guild** — archiving results and controlling canon conformity.

---

## 10.4 Interspecies Science

All three species participate in science (→ see [Volume II, Ch.14, "The Three Species"]): Kel'vash — engineering and geological directions, Moryn — oceanography and deep-sea biology. Interspecies coordination is governed by the Law of Accord.

---

## 10.5 Ethical Limits

Scientific activity is limited by an ethical code:

- **Biological caution** — restriction of risky research;
- **Ethics of sapience** — regulation of consciousness research;
- **Limited AI** — prohibition of self-aware artificial intelligence.

These limits are not a prohibition of knowledge but an institutional framework (→ see [Volume II, Ch.10, "History of Science"]).
