## Introduction

The eight domains are the territorial foundation of Xha'Ruun civilization. Each domain is a historical-cultural region with its own identity, economic specialization, and governance system. The principle of "unity without uniformity" allows the domains to retain autonomy under common Council norms.

## 5.2 Functions of the Domains

| Function | Center | Regions |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Legislation | Council Charter | Local norms |
| Taxes | Guilds | 30% — region |
| Education | Scholars Guild | Local schools |
| Healthcare | Medic Guild | Hospitals |
| Infrastructure | Builders Guild | Road network |

The distribution of functions is a model of federalism: the Council and guilds set common standards; the domains implement them locally with regard to local conditions. The tax system directs 30% of collected funds to regional budgets.

---

## 5.3 Domain Autonomy

Domain autonomy covers:

- **Culture** — languages, traditions, festivals;
- **Education** — local schools and universities;
- **Urban planning** — city layouts;
- **Local law** — norms not contradicting the canon.

The limit of autonomy — unity of measures, defense, foreign policy, and basic legislation.

---

## 5.4 Domains and Representation

Each domain delegates representatives to the **Region Chamber** of the Council of Unity (→ see [Volume III, Ch.02, "Council of Unity"]). Distribution by population: larger domains have more representatives, but no domain can block a decision alone.

---

## 5.5 Economic Specialization

- **Kharak-Thar** — administrative and scientific center;
- **Kharak-Tor** — agriculture;
- **Rhuval-Ven** — trade;
- **Rhuval-Irkhen** — industry;
- **Vexar-Shan** — technology;
- **Vexar-Mor** — energy;
- **Island** — culture and seafaring;
- **Polar** — science and resources.

Specialization ensures complementarity — the economic necessity of unity.
