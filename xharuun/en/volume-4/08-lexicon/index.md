## Introduction

The lexical system of Xha'Ruun is built on a compact root base: ~500 basic roots generate tens of thousands of derived words. The root is monosyllabic (CVC or CV); word formation is agglutinative, through suffixes with transparent semantics.

---

## 8.1 Root System

The Xha'Ruun language is based on ~500 roots. Each root is monosyllabic (CVC or CV).

| Root | Meaning | Derivatives |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **xha-** | thought, mind | xha'ruun (people), xha'ven (truth) |
| **khar-** | light, star | khar'vex (luminary), kharan (light) |
| **the-** | soil | thexar (planet), theri (soil) |
| **sha-** | language, word | sharuu (writing system), shaven (dialect) |
| **ruun-** | people | ruuna (community), ruunen (society) |

The roots are registered (L-000001–L-000500) and serve as the basis of lexicography. The polysemy of roots is a source of word-formation productivity.

---

## 8.2 Word Formation

| Method | Suffix | Example |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Noun | -∅ | **khal** (life) |
| Adjective | -an | **kharan** (light) |
| Verb | -en | **khalen** (to live) |
| Abstraction | -ven | **khalven** (vitality) |
| Agent | -sha | **khalsha** (living one) |
| Place | -the | **khalthe** (habitat) |

Each suffix has one meaning — the agglutinative principle is preserved in word formation as well. Combinations of suffixes create polysyllabic derivatives with a transparent internal form.

---

## 8.3 Compound Words

Composition is the second source of vocabulary growth:

- **xha + ruun** → xha'ruun (people — lit. "mind + people");
- **khar + vex** → khar'vex (star — "light + home");
- **the + xar** → thexar (planet — "soil + homeland").

The apostrophe marks the boundary of the components of a compound word.

---

## 8.4 Semantic Fields

Roots are grouped into semantic fields:

- **Nature** — khar (light), the (soil), mor (time), ven (truth);
- **Society** — sha (language), ruun (people), khal (life), rass (accord);
- **Action** — khalen (to live), vexen (to build), tharen (to speak).

The fields reflect the cultural priorities of the civilization (→ see [Volume I, Ch.06]).

---

## 8.5 Borrowings

The vocabulary includes limited borrowings from the languages of other species:

- **Kel'vash** — mountain and technical terms;
- **Moryn** — oceanographic terms.

Borrowings are phonologically adapted (→ see [Volume IV, Ch.02, "Phonology"]).
