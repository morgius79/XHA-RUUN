## 16.2 Magnetosphere Structure

### 16.2.1 Elements

- **Bow shock** — ~15 Rₜ sunward, Khar'Vex stellar wind (~842.6 mo/mig) collides → standing shock. Front plasma T ~5.4×10⁴°X
- **Magnetopause** — pressure balance ~12 Rₜ from center
- **Inner magnetosphere** — ~1.5 to ~12 Rₜ, dipolar (compressed dayside, stretched nightside)
- **Magnetotail** — ~80 Rₜ nightside, plasma sheet + 2 polar lobes opposite polarity

### 16.2.2 Radiation Belts

| Belt | Altitude (mo) | Composition | Energy |

| Inner | 243.9–914.6 | Proton | 0.1–100 MeV |
| Outer | 1 626.0–3 658.5 | Electron | 0.01–10 MeV |

**Inner** — stable, high-E proton, spacecraft danger. Xha'Ruun comm/nav satellite either below (40.7–203.3 mo) or above (6 097.6–8 130.1 mo).

**Outer** — variable, Khar'Vex activity. CME flare → electron ×10–100.

**Third** — seasonal, high stellar activity only.

> **Rass-Ven:** Belt intensity relatively low. Weaker field traps fewer particle → more cosmic radiation reaches surface.
