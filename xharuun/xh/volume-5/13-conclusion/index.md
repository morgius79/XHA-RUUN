## Key Ideas

1. **Culture as memory** — every element (holiday, clothing, cuisine, sport) reproduces history; transmission through the body: silence, ritual, taste, movement.
2. **The scientific method as language** — mandatory evidentiality makes the method a norm of speech; culture inherited a language in which one cannot speak otherwise.
3. **The asymmetry of ethics** — ignorance is safe, concealment is grave; losing does not lower standing, refusing a "path review" does.
4. **Six as a constant** — from sports lanes to poetic lines, from notes to calendar rites — echoing the six limbs and the six letters of the genetic code.
5. **Unity in diversity** — "unity without unification" at all levels: guild marks on an identical cut, domain styles with a common language, three species exchanging ornaments.

## Culture and History

Culture is a direct product of historical catastrophes: every ethical principle answers a concrete event (→ rhu [Thexar-V, Sha-El 12, "Ven-Rass-El"]) — the debt of knowledge to the Dark Ages, the Law of Consent to the conflicts with Kel'vash and Moryn, the ban on self-aware AI to the dispute of +2 000–2 400.

---

## Culture and Language

The language and culture are inseparable. The evidential system (→ rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 06, "Sha-Ven Rass"]) is an ethical norm reproduced with every `-ven` or `-rass`; the senary structure (→ rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 09, "Mor-Ruun Khal-sha"]) is the structure of holidays, sport, and poetry. Xha'Ruun culture is a language that became a norm.

---

## Culture and the Three Species

The three species share no single culture but exchange its elements: the Kel'vash "stone meals," the Moryn "light meals," the exchange of ornaments. The exchange is dialogue, not unification — each side preserves its identity (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 14, "Xha-Sha Xha-Mor"]).

## Ruun Ven

1. Culture is a mechanism of memory transmission through the body (ritual, taste, movement, silence).
2. The scientific method is a norm of speech, not a profession; evidentiality makes scientific honesty grammatical.
3. Ethics is asymmetric: concealment of knowledge is worse than theft; "I do not know" is safer than "I know."
4. Six is a cultural constant: from anatomy to poetry, from sport to the calendar.
5. The three species preserve their differences, exchanging ornaments and rituals, but not shared clothing.
