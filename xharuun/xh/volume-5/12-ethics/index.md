# Thexar-V: Xa-Sha-Ven — Sha-El 12: Ven-Rass-El — Ethics and Morality

> *"Ethics is not a list of rules. Ethics is a way of seeing the other. If you see yourself in him — you cannot do him harm. If you see in him a source of knowledge — you cannot destroy him. If you see in him a part of harmony — you cannot break it."*
> — From "The Three Paths of Ethics," the school of the "Three Paths," −400 Y.E.

**Volume:** 20 sha-ruun
**Mor-ven:** Ethics is not a religious doctrine — it grew out of historical experience, is fixed in the canon, and is reproduced by everyday practice. The system is **asymmetric**: ignorance is not stigmatized, concealment of knowledge is severely stigmatized; losing a dispute does not lower standing, refusing a path review does. Shaped by the memory of the Dark Ages — the ethics of a surviving species that once nearly vanished and decided it would not allow it again.

## 12.1 The Three Pillars of Ethics

| Value | Concept | Meaning | Embodiment |
|-------|---------|---------|-----------|
| **Knowledge** | **Ven** | Truth won by experience belongs to all | Debt of transmission (ven-mor) |
| **Harmony** | **Rass** | Balance of personal and social, tradition and progress | Law of Consent |
| **Truth** | **Ven-Ash** | Truthfulness as the foundation of trust | Mandatory evidentiality |

Knowledge is first — a civilization that survived the loss of its libraries placed it above all, including individual life: *"To kill a body is to take one life. To kill knowledge is to take thousands."*

---

## 12.2 The Debt of Knowledge (ven-mor)

The central category: the obligation to pass what one has learned to the archives and descendants.

| Aspect | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Formulation | "Knowledge, once acquired, must be transmitted" |
| Subject | Anyone who has acquired new knowledge or experience |
| Addressee | Guild of Keepers, district archive, students |
| Term | Until death or leaving the guild |
| Violation | Concealment of knowledge — a grave offense |

Embodied in the **District Diary** (182 districts), the **path review** in sport, **culinary apprenticeship**, and **Knowledge Day** (→ rhu [Thexar-V, Sha-El 07, "The-Ven Khal-sha"]). A scientist who carried a discovery to the grave stands lower than a thief.

---

## 12.3 The Law of Consent (rass-tor)

No decision affecting several parties is made without consulting all those affected.

| Level | Application |
|-------|-------------|
| Inter-species | The three species consult on common matters |
| Guild | Council decisions require coordination with the guilds |
| Family | A triad decides with the consent of all three members |
| Personal | Creating a mind without its consent is unethical |

**The prohibition of self-aware AI** — a direct consequence: a mind without the possibility of consenting is in a position ethics cannot justify (→ rhu [Thexar-VI, Sha-El 05, "Mor-Xha-El"]).

## 12.4 Mandatory Evidentiality

| Marker | Source | Ethical Assessment |
|--------|--------|--------------------|
| **-ven** | Direct evidence | Highest reliability |
| **-sha** | Indirect (heard, sensed) | Reliable |
| **-tor** | Inference from traces | Conjecture |
| **-rass** | Hearsay | Requires citing the source |

A **false marker** — `-ven` for what one has not seen — is a grave offense, with up to **20 years of corrective labor**. **Plagiarism** is not theft of intellectual property (absent in a post-scarcity society) but a **variety of falsehood**: a false `-ven` instead of `-rass`.

## 12.5 Asymmetries

| Action | Assessment | Opposite | Assessment |
|--------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Concealment of knowledge | Grave | Admitting ignorance | Safe |
| Lying with `-ven` | Grave | Admitting inference (`-tor`) | Normal |
| Refusal of a path review | Grave | Losing a dispute | Does not lower standing |
| Destruction of an archive | Gravest | Error in an experiment | Acceptable |
| Suppressing birth without indications | Grave | Voluntary refusal to reproduce | A right |

The formula **"Mor-ven"** ("I do not know") is socially safe — it demonstrates honesty.

## 12.9 The Ethics of Three Species

| Principle | Xha'Ri | Kel'vash | Moryn |

The **"long experiment"** — the debt of knowledge adapted to Kel'vash metabolism: transmission requires a generational relay. The **"light memory"** of the Moryn — bioluminescent records fixed on reefs and in deep archives.

## 12.10 Ethics and Economics

A post-scarcity society makes ethical norms stricter: when hunger is absent, moral choice becomes the only choice.

| Sphere | Ethical Norm |
|--------|--------------|
| Labor | Not for survival but for contribution |
| Distribution | "By need" at the base, "by contribution" above |
| Knowledge | Open, not sold |
| Land and resources | Belong to civilization |
| Archives | Accessible to all |

**Energy-backed currency** (1 VT = 1 kWh) — pegging to an inexhaustible resource (fusion) makes speculation impossible.

## 12.13 Ethical Dilemmas and Disputes

| Dilemma | Positions |
|---------|-----------|
| Cloning the sentient | Prolongation of life vs Uniqueness of the person |
| Interstellar expansion | Spreading life vs Risk of losing unity |

Disputes are not resolved by voting but **maintained as part of living culture**: an ethics without disputes is dead.
