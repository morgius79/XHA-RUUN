# Thexar-V: Xa-Sha-Ven — Sha-El 11: Thar-Mor Rass — Holidays and Calendar Rites

> *"A people without holidays is a people without memory. A people celebrating every day is a people without a future. We celebrate six times a year — and that is enough to remember who we are and not to forget why we live."*
> — From the canon of the Council of Unity, +1 800 Y.E.

**Mor-ven:** The holiday calendar is an institution where history, ethics, and cosmology meet in ritual. Few holidays — six main ones and five days of Renewal — by design: if you celebrate often, the holiday is devalued. Each holiday is not a break from work but a **reproduction of the cultural core**. The rites are not religious: society is predominantly secular, and holidays reproduce memory and ethics, not a cult.

---

## 11.1 The Calendar Foundation

The year lasts **412.7 local days** (~10 040 standard hours), 14 months of ~29.5 days, and ends with **5 days of Renewal** (Ven-Khal), belonging to no month (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 17, "Thar-Mor The-El"]).

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Months | 14 (~29.5 days each) |
| Days of Renewal | 5; every 5th year — 6 days |
| Main holidays | 6 (planetary) |
| Regional holidays | ~20 (by domain and guild) |

The days of Renewal are "time outside time" — a universal truce.

## 11.2 The Six Main Holidays

**New Year (Vek'shan) — 1 Khar-Ven.** Beginning of the year, renewal of obligations. White cloak; light dishes. **The rite of obligations**: each triad, guild, and domain repeats its obligations — a public reminder, not an oath; failing to fulfill them is a social stain.

**Unity Day (Rass-Vek) — 15 Rass-Ur.** Signing of the Treaty of Unity (−750). Six colors on one outfit; "dish of consent"; lasts 3 days. The **Treaty** (canonical, 108 lines) is read aloud in every district by a Keeper or Council member; the third day is solitary reflection (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 05, "Ven-Khal Thar-El"]).

**Remembrance Day (Mor-Ghal) — 11 Thar-Ghal.** Memory of the Red Rot (−2 120 – −2 105), which claimed up to **60%** of the Empire's population. Dark clothing without guild marks; the lean **"gray dish"** (without sulfur) — physiologically "unfinished," symbolizing irreplaceable loss. Silence until noon, then reading of names (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 04, "Thar-Xa-Khal"]).

**Knowledge Day (Ven-Thex) — 1 Ven-Khal.** Founding of the First Library (−4 800). Azure elements in all guilds; "scholar's snack." Handing in observations, the Procession of Instruments, the Dispute of Six; children may ask any master anything (→ rhu [Thexar-V, Sha-El 07, "The-Ven Khal-sha"]).

**Birth Day (Nur-Vek) — 14 The-Nur.** Birth of a new generation. Festive clothing with egg ornaments; sweet dishes. Triads present the year's larvae in the district square; elders visit schools to tell of their guilds.

**Harmony Day (Rass-Thal) — 30 Veks-An.** Preparation for Renewal, equilibrium. Neutral colors (all six equally); six dishes in six servings. **The check of debts** — public self-reflection on unfulfilled obligations (not monetary); unfulfilled obligations carry into the new year.

## 11.3 The Days of Renewal — 5 days outside the months

| Day | Name | Content |
|-----|------|---------|
| 1st | Silence | Silence, no work |
| 2nd | Purification | Cleaning, repairs, preparation |
| 3rd | Feasting | Communal meals, music |
| 4th | Exchange | Gift-giving, transmission of knowledge |
| 5th | Return | Preparation for the year, reading of plans |

The longest but most ascetic holiday. The universal truce extends even to legal proceedings: courts do not sit, disputes are postponed.

## 11.5 The Ritual Structure of a Holiday

| Phase | Time | Content |
|-------|------|---------|
| **Preparation** | 3 days before | Cleaning, food, handing in affairs |
| **Morning** | Start of the day | Rite (reading, silence, exchange) |
| **Noon** | Climax | Public action (procession, dispute, meal) |
| **Evening** | Completion | Music, art, family gatherings |
| **Night** | Silence | Return to ordinary life |

The structure is invariable: morning — rite, noon — climax, evening — completion.

---

## 11.6 Holiday and Music

New Year — choral hymns; Unity Day — the **"Symphony of the Treaty"** (written +850, full orchestra of 60 instruments, **108 minutes** = the lines of the Treaty, senary 6/8 meters); Remembrance Day — silence then low infrasonic tones; Knowledge Day — sonification of scientific series; Birth Day — children's choirs; Harmony Day — chamber music.

---

## 11.7 Holiday and Art

Painting (historical scenes on Unity Day), sculpture, theater (the **"Fall of the Empire"** — the same canonical play staged every Remembrance Day, unchanged since +1 200), poetry (the "Cycle of Times"), and resonance installations for Knowledge Day.

---

## 11.8 Holiday and Childhood

New Year — recitation of the **"children's oath"** ("I promise to learn, to ask, and not to be silent when I see injustice," ages 5–15); Unity Day — children read one line of the Treaty in turn; Remembrance Day — participation in the silence; Knowledge Day — the right to question any master; Birth Day — welcoming newborns; Harmony Day — check of children's obligations.

## 11.9 The Holiday of Three Species

| Species | Feature of Participation |
|---------|--------------------------|
| Xha'Ri | Main organizer, oral speech |
| Kel'vash | "Stone meals," vibrational communication |
| Moryn | "Light meals," bioluminescence |

The **"stone meal" of Unity Day** — the Xha'Ri and Kel'vash sit side by side but do not eat; they exchange knowledge about food through rock vibrations (→ rhu [Thexar-V, Sha-El 09, "Khal-The-El"]).

## 11.10 Prohibitions and Violations

| Prohibition | Consequence of Violation |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| Work during Renewal | Social disapproval |
| Breaking the silence on Remembrance Day | Expulsion from the square |
| Refusing to answer children on Knowledge Day | Loss of guild status for a year |
| Gift-giving without reciprocation | Disrespect for the principle of exchange |

The punishments are social, not legal: in a post-scarcity society, disapproval is punishment enough.

## 11.11 Holiday and Memory

| Holiday | Transmitted Memory |
|----------|--------------------|
| New Year | Renewal of obligations |
| Unity Day | Treaty of Unity, unification |
| Remembrance Day | Red Rot, fragility of civilization |
| Knowledge Day | First Library, value of knowledge |
| Birth Day | Continuation of the species |
| Harmony Day | Equilibrium before the transition |

Memory is not written down — it is **experienced through the body**: silence, quiet, exchange, reading.

## Ruun Ven

1. Six main holidays and five days of Renewal; the small number is a consequence of long-term orientation.
2. Each holiday reproduces the cultural core: New Year — obligations, Unity Day — the Treaty (−750), Remembrance Day — the epidemic (−2 120), Knowledge Day — the First Library (−4 800).
3. The ritual structure is uniform: morning — rite, noon — climax, evening — completion.
4. The days of Renewal — "time outside time," a universal truce.
5. A holiday transmits memory through bodily experience; the three species participate on equal terms via "stone" and "light" meals.
