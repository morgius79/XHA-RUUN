## 21.1 Classification + Sulfur Cycle

**10 terrestrial biomes**, 5 marine, 3 unique (hydrothermal vent, cave, subglacial lake).

**Sulfur Cycle — key difference:**

```
Atmosphere: H₂S 0.0008%
    ▲              │
Volcanic          │ Biogenic
emission          ▼
┌────────────────────────────────┐
│ Biosphere: H₂S → organic S     │
│ Chemosynth: H₂S + CO₂ → org    │
│ Photosynth: S-amino acids      │
└────────────────────────────────┘
    ▲              │
Mineralization    │ Assimilation
    ▼              │
Soils/Ocean: SO₄²⁻, FeS₂, H₂S
    ▲              │
Leaching          │ Volcanic
    ▼              ▼
Lithosphere: FeS₂, CaSO₄, S°, mantle S
```

H₂S participates in metabolism on par with carbon.

## 21.3 Unique Ecosystems

### 21.3.1 Equatorial Jungle (0°–8° lat, S Kharak, N Rhuval)

**5-layer structure:**

| Layer | Height | Closure | Species | Light |
|-------|--------|---------|---------|-------|
| A (emergent) | 60–85 m | <10% | 120 | Full |
| B (canopy) | 30–60 m | 85% | 2 800 | Partial |
| C (understory) | 5–30 m | 60% | 4 200 | Heavy shade |
| D (shrub) | 1–5 m | 30% | 3 800 | Low |
| E (floor) | 0–1 m | <5% | 1 200 | Minimal |

**Adaptation:** Lower-layer leaves absorb H₂S directly from air (stomata) → metabolic compensation for low light.
