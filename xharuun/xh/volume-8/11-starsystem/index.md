## Ruun Ven

1. Khar'Vex is an orange K2V dwarf of 0.82 M☉, 6.5 billion cycles old, with a calm activity profile and an expected main-sequence lifetime of ~22 billion cycles.
2. Seven planets orbit the star — Vax, Xhel, Théxar, Zhal, Rhuun, Koss, Phor — with Théxar at 0.63 AU, the optimum of a habitable zone spanning 0.48–0.92 AU.
3. The Orbital Belt (1.15–1.65 AU) holds ~2.4 million bodies >1 km and is the system's metal reserve; trial Fe-Ni mining began +3,800, full-scale development planned from +4,200.
4. Théxar's two moons divide roles: Serrin stabilizes the planetary axis and hosts the scientific base (+2,400, Ven-Khal crater), while Thel supplies He-3 at ~485,000 kx/year.
5. Orbital infrastructure is anchored by the "Khar-Tor" elevator (+3,000), 24 GNSS satellites, 72 orbital stations of four types, and the living Orbital Forest.
6. Expansion beyond the system is bounded by lightspeed; the "Sowing-1" probe (launched +3,800, 0.08 c) is the sole realistic program, carrying Xha-Vokh embryos and a knowledge archive.
