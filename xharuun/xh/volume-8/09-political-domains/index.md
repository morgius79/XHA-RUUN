**Mor-ven:** The eight political domains cover the whole of Théxar, with borders shaped by historical, cultural, and economic distinctions accumulated over millennia. Three lie on Kharak, two on Rhuval, two on Vexar, plus the Island and Polar domains. Each has its own capital, guild headquarters, and specialization within a unified planetary framework.

## Ruun Ven

1. Eight domains partition Théxar: 3 on Kharak, 2 on Rhuval, 2 on Vexar, 1 island, 1 polar.
2. Total population ~2.2 billion (Xha'ri-primary); Kharak-Thar is the largest (480M), Polar the smallest (12K).
3. Kharak-Thar dominates administration and science (24% GDP); Rhuval-Irkhen leads heavy/military industry; Vexar-Mor holds the orbital elevator.
4. Council of Unity Chamber of Regions distributes 25 seats by population, capped at 5 per domain to prevent domination.
5. Guild headquarters are spread across domains, aligning economic specialization with governance.
6. Three inter-species territories operate under consent of Xha'Ruun, Kel'vash, and Moryn representatives.
