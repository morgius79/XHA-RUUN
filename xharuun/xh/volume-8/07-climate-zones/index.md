**Mor-ven:** Climate zonation of Théxar is set by K2V low insolation, elevated CO₂, and a 14.2° axial tilt — yielding smooth transitions and muted seasons. Seven latitudinal zones from equatorial to polar are complemented by altitudinal belts and ocean moderation.

## 07.1 The Equatorial Zone (0°–12°)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Average temperature | 60.3–62.5°X |
| Precipitation | 2,500–4,000 mm/year |
| Humidity | 75–95% |
| T amplitude (annual) | ±2.1°X |

Humid tropical forests on southern Kharak and northern Rhuval. No dry season; even precipitation. Biodiversity up to 12,000 plant species per 10 mo².

**Key regions:**
- The Thar Delta (Kharak) — 0.03 million mo² of jungle
- Northern Rhuval — 0.08 million mo² of hylea

## 07.2 The Tropical Zone (12°–28°)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Average temperature | 54.9–60.3°X |
| Precipitation | 800–2,000 mm/year (seasonal) |
| Humidity | 50–70% |
| Dry season | 3–5 months |

Savannas and open woodlands; weak seasonality due to small axial tilt. Grasslands with forest islands.

**Key regions:**
- Central Plain of Kharak — 0.34 million mo²
- Savannas of Rhuval — 0.21 million mo²

## 07.3 The Subtropical Zone (28°–38°)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Average temperature | 51.6–55.9°X |
| Precipitation | 500–1,200 mm/year |
| Humidity | 40–60% |

Mediterranean-type climate: warm dry summers, mild wet winters. Most favorable zone for agriculture.

**Key regions:**
- Coast of the Serrin Sea (Kharak)
- Western coast of Rhuval
- Northern coast of Vexar

## 07.4 The Temperate Zone (38°–54°)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Average temperature | 47.3–52.6°X |
| Precipitation | 500–900 mm/year |
| Seasonal amplitude | ±8.2°X |

Broadleaf and mixed forests. Four seasons with small temperature swing. Main settlement zone of the Xha'Ruun.

**Key regions:**
- Southeastern Kharak — 0.41 million mo² of forest
- Central Vexar — 0.28 million mo²

## 07.5 The Subpolar Zone (54°–66°)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Average temperature | 42.4–47.3°X |
| Precipitation | 200–500 mm/year |
| Snow cover | 4–6 months |

Taiga and tundra. Low population density; main source of forest resources.

**Key regions:**
- Northern taiga of Kharak — 0.47 million mo²
- Southern tundras of Rhuval — 0.12 million mo²

## 07.6 The Polar Zone (66°–90°)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Average temperature | 31.5–42.4°X |
| Precipitation | 50–200 mm/year |
| Ice cover | Permanent |

Polar deserts and ice caps. Population — research stations only. Polar caps: 0.17 million mo² (northern), 0.13 million mo² (southern).

**Research stations:**
- Ven-Deep (North Pole) — 800 people
- Khar-Ice (South Pole) — 400 people

## 07.7 Altitudinal Zonation

Climate zones repeat vertically: every 305 sha of height, temperature falls by ~2.0°X.

| Belt | Height (sha) | Climatic analogue |
|------|--------------|-------------------|
| Plain | 0–610 | Equatorial–tropical |
| Foothill | 610–1,829 | Subtropical |
| Mountain | 1,829–3,659 | Temperate |
| Subalpine | 3,659–5,488 | Subpolar |
| Alpine | 5,488–7,317 | Polar |
| Nival | >7,317 | Glaciers |

**Example:** the Khar-Tor ridge includes all belts from tropical to nival. Highest point — Mt. Verksan (9,537 sha) — is ice-covered year-round.

## 07.8 The Influence of the Oceans

| Factor | Thalas (Northern) | Gharra (Southern) |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Water temperature (eq.) | 62.5°X | 60.3°X |
| Water temperature (pol.) | 44.0°X | 42.0°X |
| Influence on climate | Softening of winters on Kharak | Moderate influence on Rhuval |

The cold Thel current along eastern Kharak creates a drier climate. The warm Khar-Vex current along the western coast intensifies precipitation.

## Ruun Ven

1. Seven climate zones span from equatorial (62.5°X) to polar (31.5°X).
2. The 14.2° axial tilt smooths seasonal contrasts across all latitudes.
3. Altitudinal zonation mirrors latitudinal zonation; ~2.0°X drop per 305 sha.
4. Oceans Thalas and Gharra moderate climate; Thel and Khar-Vex currents create local differences.
5. Subtropical zone is the most favorable for agriculture; temperate zone is the main Xha'Ruun settlement zone.
6. Polar caps total 0.30 million mo²; permanent ice, population limited to Ven-Deep and Khar-Ice stations.
