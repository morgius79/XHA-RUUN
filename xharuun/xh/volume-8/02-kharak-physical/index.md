## Ruun Ven

1. Kharak is the oldest continent of Théxar — 34.2 million мо² of eroded, low-relief landmass.
2. Its highest point, Mt. Verksan, reaches only 1,590 мо; average elevation is 620 мо.
3. The Khar-Tor river (870 мо) is the continent's longest waterway and gives its name to the central ridge.
4. Lake Mor-Ven (8,700 мо²) is the largest inland water body.
5. The megacity Khar-Vexar (18.6 million) is Kharak's demographic anchor and the world's prime meridian.
6. Terrain grades north→south: taiga → Serrin Plateau → Khar-Tor ridge → Central Plain → Shan Desert.
