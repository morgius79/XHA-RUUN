# Thexar-IX: Sha-Ruun — Sha-El 04: Rass-El Sha-Ruun — Society: Lexicon

> *"A people with no word for 'together' is doomed to live apart. The Xha'Ruun have nine such words."*
> — Lexicographer Ven-Thara, Keeper Guild

---

**Ruun-ak rass:** → rhu [Thexar-IX, Sha-El 02, "Thel-ak Ruun"], → rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 01, "Government"], → rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 08, "History of the Guilds"]
**Volume:** 30 sha-ruun
**Mor-ven:** The social lexicon is built around the root **rass** (unity), not the root **thar** (force); coercion is always expressed as separation from the collective through **tor** ("away"). Seventy-one entries span power, guilds, law, economy, family, and social roles.
