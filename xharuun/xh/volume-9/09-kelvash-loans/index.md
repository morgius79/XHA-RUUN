## 09.1 Context

The **Kel'vash** are a silicon-organic sapient species of the highlands and deserts: solvent H₂SO₄, optimal temperature 180–220 °X, chemoautotrophic metabolism (→ rhu [Thexar-VII, Sha-El 06, "Kel'vash-mor Xa-Sha"]). Their language rests on **vibrational-mineral articulation** — resonance in the crystalline plates of the skull rather than a stream of air.

Hence a fundamental problem: **Kel'vash phonology cannot be rendered in Xha'Ruun**. Every loanword is not a transcription but an **approximation**, ratified by the Guild of Scribes.

There are only **31** loans in total, all in geology, high-temperature chemistry, and mining engineering. None concerns an abstract notion: Xha'Ruun does not borrow what it can compose.

## 09.2 Rules of Adaptation

| Rule | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Cluster reduction | Kel'vash permits C₅; Xha'Ruun at most C₂ | *krzthal* → **karthal** |
| Epenthetic ə | a neutral vowel is inserted into a forbidden cluster | *vshk* → **vəshak** |
| Sibilant substitution | Kel'vash /ʂ/, /ʐ/ → Xha'Ruun /ʃ/ | *ʐel* → **shel** |
| Tone loss | the five-tone Kel'vash system → neutral tone | all loans |
| Obligatory suffix | a loan may not stand as a bare root | +**-en**, +**-ak**, +**-in** |
| Initial stress | per Xha'Ruun norm, not the source's | **ká**-thren |

The last rule is decisive: once suffixed, a loan **enters the agglutinative system** and inflects like a native word. The Kel'vash root *kathr* yields a full paradigm: kathren-sha, kathren-thar, kathren-mor…

## 09.6 Disputed Cases

| Form | Dispute | Position of the Guild of Scribes |
|------|---------|----------------------------------|
| **kel'vash** | loan or composition? | the species' autonym; the apostrophe admitted as an exception |
| **ghar** | from Kel'vash *ghr* "darkness," or a native root? | ruled **native**; the resemblance is coincidental |
| **thal** | from Kel'vash *thl* "below"? | ruled native (from *thalas*, ocean) |
| **vurth** | admit as **vurthen** or calque as "sulfur crystal"? | **vurthen** adopted — the calque is longer |
| **kathr** | may a bare root be admitted? | **no**; a suffix is required |

The fifth case set the general norm, in force since +2,600 Y.E.: **every loan takes a suffix at its first recording**. Without one, the form counts as a citation in a foreign tongue and is set in italics in Xha'Ruun texts.
