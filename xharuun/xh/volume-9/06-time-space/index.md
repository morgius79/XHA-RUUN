# Thexar-IX: Sha-Ruun — Sha-El 06: Mor-Ven Sha-Ruun — Time and Space

> *"We have four verb tenses, but six words for 'now.' The moment matters more than eternity — eternity is not going anywhere."*
> — From the instructions of the Keeper Guild

---

**Ruun-ak rass:** → rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 05, "Verbs and Tenses"], → rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 34, "Théxar-mor The-ven Khal-sha-ak"], → rhu [Thexar-V, Sha-El 11, "Thar-Mor Rass"]
**Volume:** 30 sha-ruun
**Mor-ven:** The spatiotemporal system is built on a **senary base**: 36 morin to a morsha, 36 morsha to a morak, 216 mormor to a century. Cardinal directions are named for observable phenomena, not abstract axes: east is "the giving side" of the rising star, north the side of the polar aurora.
