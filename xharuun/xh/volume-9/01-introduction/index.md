**Mor-ven:** The Xha'Ruun lexicon is organized by thematic sections. Each entry records the root or word in transliteration, its part of speech, translation, etymology, and an example of use. This introduction defines the grammar reference that governs every entry in the dictionary.

---

## 01.1 Structure of the Dictionary

Each entry contains:

- **Root/word** — in Xha'Ruun (transliteration)
- **Part of speech** — root, noun, verb, adjective
- **Translation** — into common usage
- **Etymology** — origin (if known)
- **Example** — usage in context
