## 11.1 How to Read This Section

Xha'Ruun is an **SOV** language (subject — object — verb) with obligatory evidentiality (→ rhu [Thexar-IX, Sha-El 07, "Sha-Ruun Ven-El"]). In the phrases below the evidential suffix is set apart: change it and you alter not the politeness but the **accountability** of what you said.

| Marker | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **-ven** | I saw it myself (direct) |
| **-sha** | I sensed it (indirect) |
| **-tor** | I infer it (inference) |
| **-rass** | I was told (reportative) |

An evidential error is the commonest mistake of learners and the most conspicuous to a native. To say *"it is raining-ven"* without having looked out the window is not an imprecision but a **lie**.

## 11.6 Formulas of Council and Court

| Xha'Ruun | Translation | Context |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **Rass-vokh-sha khal-en-ven.** | "The Council of Unity is in session." | opening a session |
| **Ven-ak-thar vex-a-khar.** | "Let the testimonies be presented." | judicial formula |
| **Sha-ven-ven, sha-ven-rass?** | "Did you see it yourself, or were you told?" | the key question in examination |
| **Ven-en-ven — venar-en.** | "What is witnessed directly is law." | legal maxim |
| **Rass-el-mor.** | "May unity abide forever." | closing a session |
| **Tor-khal-torel.** | "Denied finally." | verdict without appeal |

The third formula — **Sha-ven-ven, sha-ven-rass?** — underlies the whole of Xha'Ruun judicial procedure. It is impossible in languages without obligatory evidentiality: there the difference between "saw" and "heard" must be extracted by questioning, whereas here it has **already been uttered** along with the testimony (→ rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 09, "Vokh-Shan Khal-sha"]).
