## 13.2 Three Facts

**Composition over borrowing.** Of ~50,000 words, only **57** are loans — 31 from Kel'vash, 26 from Moryn, each ratified by the Guild of Scribes and each carrying a suffix at first recording. Everything else is built from sixteen core roots strung together by forty-one affixes in a rigid order (→ rhu [Thexar-IX, Sha-El 07, "Sha-Ruun Ven-El"]).

**Witness is grammatical.** Evidentiality cannot be omitted: every verb states the source of knowledge. The language shaped the law ("What is witnessed directly is law") and resisted — once — the Moryn's authorless speech by admitting a fifth, non-canonical suffix **-nal** (→ rhu [Thexar-IX, Sha-El 10, "Moryn-mor Sha-Ruun"]).

**A name is a sentence.** Khar'Vex is "Giver of Light," Théxar "Land of the Living" — and an untranslatable name is a scribal error. Naming is destiny, and transparency is a norm (→ rhu [Thexar-IX, Sha-El 08, "Ven-Khal Sha-Ruun"]).
