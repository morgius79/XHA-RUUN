# Thexar-IX: Sha-Ruun — Sha-El 07: Sha-Ruun Ven-El — Grammatical Affixes

> *"The root is what you say. The affix is how you answer for it."*
> — Grammarian Sharin-El, on the system of evidentiality

---

**Ruun-ak rass:** → rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 03, "Grammar Overview"], → rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 04, "Noun Cases"], → rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 06, "Evidentiality"]
**Volume:** 30 sha-ruun
**Mor-ven:** Xha'Ruun is agglutinative: meanings are strung onto the root by suffixes in a **strict order** — ROOT + derivation + number + case + tense + evidentiality. Forty-one affixes cover cases, numbers, tenses, evidentiality, derivation, and modality; the order is rigidly fixed and admits no permutation.

---

## 07.1 General Principle

```
ROOT + [derivation] + [number] + [case] + [tense] + [evidentiality]
```

Each suffix retains its form and meaning independently of its neighbors — the distinction of agglutination from inflection. A word of six morphemes reads unambiguously.

**Example of a full chain:**
**khal-ar-ak-sha-en-ven** = "those who act (two of them), in the ergative, in the present, and I see it myself."

## 07.3 Number Suffixes (3)

| Number | Suffix | Example | Translation |
|--------|--------|---------|-------------|
| Singular | -∅ | xhari | a sapient being |
| Dual | -sha | xhari-sha | two sapient beings |
| Plural | -ak | xhari-ak | sapient beings (many) |

The dual is **obligatory** for paired objects (eyes, hands, spouses, the moons Serrin and Thel) — plural in place of dual is a gross error.

## 07.4 Tense Suffixes (4)

| Tense | Suffix | Example | Translation |
|-------|--------|---------|-------------|
| Past | -ak | khal-ak | acted |
| Present | -en | khal-en | acts |
| Future | -mor | khal-mor | will act |
| Timeless | -el | khal-el | always acts (a truth) |

The timeless **-el** marks natural laws, mathematics, and canonical truths: *Khar'Vex kharel* — "Khar'Vex shines" (an unchanging property, not an observation).

## 07.5 Evidential Suffixes (4, obligatory)

| Type | Suffix | Meaning | Use |
|------|--------|---------|-----|
| Direct | -ven | "I saw/heard it myself" | personal observation |
| Indirect | -sha | "I sensed it, felt it" | indirect perception |
| Inferential | -tor | "I conclude from traces" | inference from evidence |
| Reportative | -rass | "I was told" | another's testimony |

Omitting evidentiality is **grammatically impossible** — a verb without it is unfinished. In judicial practice, changing the suffix changes the legal weight of testimony.

## 07.6 Derivational Suffixes

| Suffix | Meaning | Example | Translation |
|--------|---------|---------|-------------|
| **-ar** | bearer, agent | vexal-ar | worker |
| **-en** | process, state | vex-en | production |
| **-in** | diminutive, singulative | xar-in | child |
| **-ak** | collective, multitude | venmor-ak | archive |
| **-i** | quality, property | khar-i | shining |
| **-a** | verbal stem | vex-a | to create |
| **-an** | relational adjective | khar-an | luminous |
| **-o** | result of an action | vex-o | creation |
| **-ri** | possessing the trait | xha-ri | sapient |
| **-el** | abstraction, unity | ven-el | truthfulness |

## 07.7 Negation and Modality

| Affix | Type | Meaning | Example |
|-------|------|---------|---------|
| **tor-** | prefix | negation | tor-khal-en (does not act) |
| **-torel** | suffix | absolute negation | khal-torel (never acts) |
| **-khar** | suffix | optative | khal-khar (let it act) |
| **-thar** | suffix | obligation | khal-thar (must act) |
| **-ven?** | suffix | interrogative | khal-ven? (does it act?) |
| **-sha-el** | suffix | possibility | khal-sha-el (can act) |

Negation via **tor-** ("away, from") is the language's only prefix; every other affix is suffixal.
