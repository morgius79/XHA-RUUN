## 7.2 Pre-Civilization (−12 000 – −9 800)

### 7.2.1 Origin

### 7.2.2 First Settlement

| Settlement | Region | Date | Pop (est.) | Sha |

| Thar-Vex | Thar Valley, Kharak | −11 500 | ~200 | Oldest |
| Khar-Ur | Serrin Coast, Kharak | −11 200 | ~150 | Fishing |

### 7.2.3 Domestication
