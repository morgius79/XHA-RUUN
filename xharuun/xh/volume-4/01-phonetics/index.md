## 1.2 Articulatory Features

Features related to physiology:

- **Tones** — three pairs of vocal cords → up to five distinguishing tones
- **Overtones** — capable of two tones simultaneously (used in music and religion)
- **Uvular x** — a "guttural" fricative, characteristic of the language
- **Glottal stop ʔ** — a morpheme separator preventing vowel contraction

---

## 1.3 Syllable Structure

Predominantly open (CV). Allowed structures:

- CV — basic
- CVC — with a final sonorant (m, n, ŋ, r, l)
- CCV — with an initial cluster (p, b, k, g + sonorant)

Forbidden clusters resolved by epenthesis of ə. Stress is tonic, falling on a long vowel or the first syllable.

## 1.4 Sound and Writing

Phonetic inventory directly linked to the writing system (→ rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 07, "Sharuu Writing System"]): the syllabary reflects the syllable structure, and tones are conveyed by diacritics.
