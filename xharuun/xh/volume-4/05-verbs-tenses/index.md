# Thexar-IV: The-Sha-Ruun — Sha-El 05: Khal-ven Thar — Verbs and Tense

> *"The verb is the engine of the sentence. It carries action, tense, and the source of information. Without a verb, there is no utterance."*
> — Grammar of Xha'Ruun, Academy

---

**Ruun-ak rass:** → rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 03, "Khal-sha Rass"], → rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 06, "Evidentiality"]
**Volume:** 25 sha-ruun
**Mor-ven:** Agglutinative verbal system with rich inflectional morphology. The verb carries action, tense, and — necessarily — the source of information (evidentiality). Conjugation: tense (4), number (3), evidentiality (4, mandatory). No person agreement — person optionally by pronominal clitics.

---

## 5.1 Conjugation

Verbs conjugate for:

- **Tense** (4 types)
- **Number** (3: singular, dual, plural)
- **Evidentiality** (4 types — mandatory)

The subject does not agree in person — person is optionally expressed by pronominal clitics. Evidentiality mandatory: the speaker is obliged to indicate the source of information (→ rhu [Thexar-IV, Sha-El 06, "Evidentiality"]).

---

## 5.2 Tense System

| Tense | Suffix | Example | Translation |
|-------|--------|---------|-------------|
| Past | -ak | **khal-ak** | did |
| Present | -en | **khal-en** | does |
| Future | -mor | **khal-mor** | will do |
| Gnomic | -el | **khal-el** | does (always) |

The gnomic tense (-el) marks truths outside of time: laws, generalizations, canon. Widely used in sacred texts and scientific formulations.

---

## 5.3 Suffix Order

The verbal form is built according to the scheme:

```
Stem + tense + number + evidentiality
```

Example: **khal-en-ak-ven** = "does (plural, I see)". The suffix order is fixed and mandatory.

---

## 5.4 Aspect and Voice

Besides tense, the verb can express:

- **Aspect** — perfective/imperfective (suffix -thal for resultativity)
- **Voice** — active/passive (passive -vex-thar)
- **Causative** — the suffix -khal-ven

Optional categories; attach between the stem and the tense suffix.

---

## 5.5 Example

**Xha'ruun-sha the-thar khal-en-ven**  
People-ERG soil-ACC do-NPST-DIR  
"The people cultivate the soil (I see it)"
