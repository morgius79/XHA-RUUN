## 8.1 Origin

Guild system arose in First Empire as craft associations by profession. Workshops controlled quality, craft monopoly, transfer of mastery.

Decisive turn after the fall (**−2 150**): in dark ages the workshops became centers for preservation of knowledge and professional skills. When imperial institutions collapsed, workshops + corporations remained the only structure maintaining craft continuity. Guilds acquired functions beyond economics: keepers of knowledge in the literal sense (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 04, "Thar-Xa-Khal"]).

## 8.2 Formation of the System

| Period | Development |
|--------|-------------|
| −6 150 – −2 150 | Craft associations (First Empire) |
| −2 150 – −750 | Guilds as centers of knowledge |
| −750 – +850 | Guilds as economic unions |
| +850 – +1 500 | Formal recognition by Council of Unity |
| +1 500 – present | Modern structure (12 guilds) |

Each stage added new functions. Recovery era (−750 – +850): economic unions controlling trade + production. Council of Unity (+850): official recognition + political representation — **24 of 49** seats assigned to guilds.

## 8.4 Functions of a Guild

1. **Education** — professional training "apprentice → journeyman → master"; years, includes craft + ethics
2. **Court** — internal jurisdiction: professional disputes, quality standards, discipline
3. **Social protection** — support in illness, old age, adversity; guild "accepts the name" and accompanies to end of life
4. **Representation** — delegation to Guild Chamber of Council of Unity

Fullness of functions → guild is not a "place of work" but institution of socialization in full sense — hence epigraph: "The guild is everything."

---

## 8.5 The Guild and the State

Mutual control between guilds and Council:

- Guilds delegate **24 of 49 members**
- Regions (domains) delegate **25 members** — balance prevents either side dominating
- Key decisions require consent of both chambers
- Keeper of Knowledge controls canon conformity

Deliberate institutional choice: guilds = horizontal (professional) cross-section, regions = vertical (territorial) one. Equilibrium → 3 000 years of political stability (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 06]).

---

## 8.6 Influence and Criticism

Contradictions of the system:

- **Risk of corporate closure** — guilds can become closed elites restricting access
- **Conservatism of standards** — quality control sometimes hampers innovation
- **Distribution of influence** — largest guilds (Agronomists 5.6 M; Merchants 4.2 M) carry more weight than small (Scribes 0.5 M; Warriors 0.6 M)

Modern practice mitigates via external audit, open training access, rotation of delegates. Guilds' place in society remains a living topic of political thought.
