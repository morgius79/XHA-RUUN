## 14.2 Xha'ri — The Dominant Species

First sapient species to reach civilization (~15 000 y ago). History detailed in previous Volume II chapters. Land habitat, three-sex biology, six-limb symmetry → cultural trajectory — building "upward, toward the stars" (→ rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 05, "Xha'Ruun Sha-Mor"]).

## 14.3 Kel'vash — The Silicon-Organic Species

### 14.3.1 Habitat

Highlands and deserts — extreme environments, low humidity, high radiation. Biochemistry based on **silicon-organic compounds** → habitat unsuitable for Xha'ri and vice versa — natural barrier that reduced competition (→ rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 38, "Kel'vash"]).

### 14.3.2 Discovery and Contact

| Event | Year |
|-------|------|
| First evidence (rock paintings) | ~−8 000 |
| First confirmed contact | −2 400 |
| Recognition of sapience | −2 200 |
| Entry into the Council of Unity | +1 200 |

First contact (−2 400) = result of Xha'ri mountain expansion into Kel'vash regions. Recognition of sapience (−2 200) = legal event: equal rights, attacks prohibited.

### 14.3.3 Role in the Civilization

Specialize as engineers and builders:

- Tunnels and underground structures
- Mountain mining
- Architecture in extreme conditions

Endurance + patience → indispensable in long-term infrastructure projects, incl. mountain section of the orbital elevator.

## 14.4 Moryn — The Ocean Sapients

### 14.4.1 Habitat

Deep-water zones of the Gharra ocean (500–3 000 m). Civilization built on **bioluminescent communication** — in dark depths, light replaced sound (→ rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 39, "Moryn"]).

### 14.4.2 Discovery and Contact

| Event | Year |
|-------|------|
| First observations (from boats) | ~−3 000 |
| Confirmed contact | −1 200 |
| Establishment of permanent contact | −800 |
| Entry into the Council of Unity | +1 500 |

### 14.4.3 Communication Problems

Three fundamental barriers:

- **The language barrier** — Xha'ri sound speech vs Moryn bioluminescence
- **Different perception of time** — Moryn metabolism ~3× slower, their "minute" longer
- **Different ethical systems** — ideas about life, property, consent did not coincide

Overcoming took centuries → first two-way communication devices: light projectors and the "language of light" (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 12, "Khal-Sha The-El"]).

## 14.5 The Principle of Three: The Law of Accord

Council of Unity founded on the **principle of three** (Law of Accord): no decision affecting all species without consultation of Xha'ri, Kel'vash, Moryn.

Legal core of interspecies coexistence:

- **Equality of species** before the law
- **The right of veto** for each species on questions affecting it
- **Mandatory consultation** on common decisions

Developed through difficult centuries of contact → unique ethical construction — perhaps the most significant contribution of Théxar's civilization to the idea of sapient life in the universe (→ rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 40, "Three Species"]).
