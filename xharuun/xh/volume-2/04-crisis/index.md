## 4.1 Causes of the Fall

Not external conquest — ecological + climatic + social factors.

### 4.1.1 Ecological Collapse

Intensive farming without crop rotation (Central Plain):
- Soil depletion: **−40% humus** over 1 000 y
- Salinization of irrigated valleys
- **−60% yields** by era end
- −2 150 famine ~1 M deaths

By −2 200 grain deficit offset by Rhuval imports → drained treasury.

### 4.1.2 Climate Shift

Cooling **−2.5°C** ~200 y (≈−2 250 – −2 050), volcanic winters.

| Year | Event | T drop | Consequences |
|------|-------|--------|--------------|
| −2 220 | Tor-6 eruption | −1.5°C | 3-y crop failure |
| −2 180 | Raal-1 eruption | −2.8°C | Famine, epidemics |
| −2 150 | Crisis maximum | −3.0°C | Fall of Empire |

Double stress: anthropogenic depletion + natural cooling → yields below survival.

### 4.1.3 Social Factors

- Elite/population gap (2% vs majority)
- Bureaucracy corruption
- Rising taxes amid falling yields
- Uprisings −2 180, −2 160

Elite consumed at imperial standards while provinces starved → legitimacy destroyed faster than crop failures.

## 4.2 The Fall (−2 150, EV-0006)

1. **Spring −2 150:** hunger riot, capital Khar-Vexar
2. **Summer:** Emperor Verkmyraen V flees south
3. **Autumn:** capital destroyed by rebels
4. **Winter:** Empire → 24 independent states

**Last emperor Verkmyraen V** killed fleeing. No heir restored power. Capital destruction = imperial archives lost; surviving Code copies (temples/libraries) → core of later legal tradition.

---

## 4.3 Dark Ages (−2 150 – −750)

### 4.3.1 Demographic Decline

Kharak: ~18 M → ~4 M (**−75%**). Famine, epidemics, migration to coasts/highlands.

### 4.3.2 Loss of Knowledge

- Writing survived ~40% of regions
- Imperial Code lost 600 y (Irkhen Library only)
- Metallurgy regressed to bronze
- Intercontinental trade ceased

Selective loss: economic skills everywhere; administrative/scientific → few centers.

### 4.3.3 Centers of Preservation

| Center | Region | Preserved |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Thar Temple | Thar Valley | Writing, astronomy |
| Ven Monastery | Serrin Plateau | Medicine, botany |
| Irkhen Library | Irkhen Island | Imperial Code |
| Raal School | Raal Plateau | Metallurgy |

Framework for Second Unity intellectual tradition.

### 4.3.4 "Red Rot" Pandemic

−2 120 – −2 105: **~200 M deaths planet-wide** (vs Kharak pop ~18 M). Finished imperial remnants, accelerated disintegration.

> **Rass-Ven:** Long-term legacy — entrenched fear of biological disasters → strict sanitary norms + ethics limiting biological research.

## 4.4 Recovery (−1 500 – −750)

| Period | Process |
|--------|---------|
| −1 500 – −1 200 | Agriculture restoration |
| −1 200 – −900 | Interregional trade resumes |
| −900 – −750 | Regional union formation |

By **−800**: **8 large regional unions** (Kharak) — basis of Unity Treaty. Economic cores: Thar Valley (admin), Serrin coast (trade), Irkhen (industry), Raal (mining), Khar-Tor (farming), Shan (energy), Vexar (tech), Serrin-Ur (culture) (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 05]).

## 4.5 Legacy

1. **Limits of extensive growth** — ecologically blind resource model self-destructs → Growth Commandment (Era of Unity)
2. **Value of preserving knowledge** — monasteries/libraries → prototype of Keeper guilds
3. **Political experience of fragmentation** — 1 400 y of disunity → striving for unity → Unity Treaty
