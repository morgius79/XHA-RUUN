## 16.3 The Growth Commandment

Modern demographic regime regulated by the **Growth Commandment**: growth must not exceed **0.3% per year**. Mechanism = cultural norm + economic planning, not coercion:

- Family policy encourages stability rather than maximizing births
- Education and medicine make family planning accessible
- Post-scarcity economy removes economic incentives for large families

Direct response to the ecological crisis of the First Empire (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 04, "Thar-Xa-Khal"]): the civilization consciously limits numbers so as not to repeat the mistake of exhausting the resource base.
