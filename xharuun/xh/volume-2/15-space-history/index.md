Key milestone — **orbital elevator** (+3 000, "Khar-Tor"): radically reduced cost of delivering cargo to orbit → space infrastructure economically viable (→ rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 09, "Thar-Vek Rass-Ven"]). Second elevator under construction (completion ~+4 080).

---

## 15.3 Exploration of the Moons

### 15.3.1 Serrin

First landing on Serrin (+1 200) — most important stage of near-space exploration. Serrin, largest moon, became a base for research programs and, in the future, resource extraction.

### 15.3.2 Thel

Thel — a C-type asteroid rich in helium-3. First landing (+2 100), permanent base (+3 300, "He-3-1"), automation of extraction → planetary-scale energy resource: most of the He-3 for fusion energy comes from its surface (→ rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 33, "Khar-mor rass Thel-ak-mor Khal-vex"]).

---

## 15.4 Beyond the System

In **+3 800** an **interstellar probe** was launched toward the nearest star (5.2 ly). As of +4 028 it continues its flight — the first artificial object to leave the limits of the Khar'Vex system (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 07, "Xha-Thar-El"]).

Projects to expand the Xhel base (+4 200) and the second elevator (~+4 080) indicate the direction of the next stage — turning the Khar'Vex system into a unified industrial zone.
