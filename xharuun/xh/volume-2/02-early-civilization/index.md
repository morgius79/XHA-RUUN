## 2.1 Invention of Writing

**−9 780** — proto-writing, **independent in 3 regions**: Thar Valley (Kharak), Rhuval coast, S Vexar. Oldest + most developed = Thar Valley.

3 independent inventions → societies dense/complex enough to need fixing info; not an accident but social-evolution product.

| Stage | Period | Type | Example |
|-------|--------|------|---------|
| Pictograms | −9 800 – −9 500 | Object drawings | grain |
| Ideograms | −9 500 – −9 200 | Abstract signs | god/spirit |
| Syllabographs | −9 200 – −8 500 | Syllabic signs | xha |
| Logograms | −8 500 | Word-signs | Xha'Ruun |

Accumulation, not replacement → mixed syllabo-logographic system → classical Sha'Ruun script (→ rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 06]).

## 2.2 First Cities

Sedentism + surplus + written accounting → **city**.

| City | Founded | Population (peak) | Significance |
|------|---------|-------------------|--------------|
| Thar-Vex | ~−11 500 | — | Oldest settlement, protocity |
| **Khar-Vexar** | **−7 800** | 18.6 M (current) | **Oldest continuously inhabited** |
| Thar-Mor | −7 200 | 2.3 M | Religious center |
| Ven-Khal | −6 800 | 12.4 M (current) | Trade center Rhuval |

**Khar-Vexar** — founded −7 800 atop Thar-Vex; first true city (temples, bureaucracy, fortifications). Continuously inhabited since — oldest on Théxar (EV-0003, LOC-0001).

Cities arose at intersection of: fertile valley + trade routes + cult center. Thar-Mor = religious center (early priestly schools → Teaching of Vibration, → rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 10]).

## 2.3 Crafts and Metallurgy

| Material | Appearance | Application |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| Obsidian, flint | pre-civ era | Work tools |
| Pottery | ~−9 500 | Storage, firing |
| Copper | **−9 200** (Kharak mines) | Tools, ornaments, ritual |
| Bronze | end of era | Alloys begin |

**−9 200 copper** = technological threshold. Specialized craftsmen = first professional group outside food production → required surplus + redistribution → accelerated stratification.

---

## 2.4 Social Stratification

Mid-era: kin group → **territorial** (neighboring) community.

- **Priesthood** — ritual control + knowledge (via writing)
- **Military leaders** — settlement protection, retinues
- **Elder-administrators** — surplus + irrigation management

**Theocratic chiefdom**: high priest-leader = cult + justice + distribution in one. → precondition for emperor institution (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 03, "The First Empire"]).

---

## 2.5 Trade Networks (by end of era)

- **Obsidian** — Vexar volcanic massifs → Kharak + Rhuval
- **Copper** — Kharak mines
- **Pottery + ornaments** — prestige goods

Standardized weights (±2% identical mass across settlements) → agreed measurement system before unified calendar. Routes: riverine (Thar) + coastal (boats).

---

## 2.6 Cults + Temples

- **Resonator temple** Thar-Mor (prototype Khar-Vokh-Thel) — acoustic structures for collective singing
- **Vexar cave shrines** — pre-civ continuity
- **Ritual pottery** with celestial symbols — early moon cult

Cults systematize → common pantheon, demiurge **Khar-Vex** supreme. → groundwork for Teaching of Vibration.

---

## 2.7 End of Era — First Empire Preconditions

By −6 200: cities, writing, metallurgy, priesthood, trade, codified customs. Missing: political union.

**Code of Khar (≈−7 200)** — oldest known written legal code: 40 articles on stone stele in Khar-Vexar. Regulated property, marriage (triads), punishments, debts. First step custom → law.

First Empire founded **−6 150** (EV-0004).
