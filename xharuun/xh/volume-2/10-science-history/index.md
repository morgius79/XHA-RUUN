## 10.1 The Pre-Scientific Era

**−9 800 – −750.** Accumulation of empirical knowledge without theoretical base: observations recorded but not systematized.

| Achievement | Time | Significance |
|-------------|------|--------------|
| Calendar | −8 000 | 14 months × ~29.5 days |
| Astronomy | −7 000 | Prediction of eclipses |
| Medicine | −6 000 | Herbal treatment |
| Mathematics | −5 500 | Six-base system |
| Geometry | −4 000 | Land surveying |

Outstanding achievements: **six-base mathematics** (Sha-Ruun, −5 500) and **atomic theory** (Ven-Tal, −5 200), from the Era of Great Discoveries. First circumnavigation (Gharra-Vex, −4 200) proved Théxar sphericity — first major theoretical conclusion confirmed by practice (→ rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 28, "Théxar Xha-Sha Mor"]).

## 10.2 The Era of Discovery (−750 – +1 000)

Systematic scientific method: observation → experiment → theory. Science takes shape as an institution, separated from religion.

| Name | Years | Discovery |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Vel-Shar | −500 – −450 | Magnetism, compass |
| Khal-Ven | +80 – +120 | Circumference of Théxar |

First scientific societies created (predecessors of Scholars Guild); Academy of Khar-Vexar = center of systematic knowledge.

## 10.3 The Industrial-Thermonuclear Revolution (−1 200 – +2 000)

| Year | Discovery | Scientist/Engineer |
|------|-----------|--------------------|
| −1 200 | Steam engine | Thal-Ven |
| +1 200 | Fusion (first demonstration) | Khal-Shar |
| +1 420 | First industrial fusion reactor | — |
| +1 500 | Bio-optical processor | Ven-Mor |
| +1 800 | Quantum communication | Ru-Khal |

Two revolutions defined modernity. **Industrial** (steam engine, −1 200): machine energy accessible. **Thermonuclear** (+1 420): practically inexhaustible. Together → economy of scarcity → economy of abundance (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 09, "Khal-Ven The-El"]).

## 10.4 Modern Science

By 4 028 E.U.:

- 240 research institutes
- 8.4 million scientists (all three species)
- R&D spending: 7.2% of GDP
- 12 hydrothermal vent fields under permanent observation

Organized on guild principle: Scholars Guild coordinates research program, Keepers Guild — archives + canon compliance. Science remains open: results published in unified access system, most important discoveries undergo collegial review.

---

## 10.5 Science and Ethics

Conscious restriction of dangerous directions:

- **Restriction of biological research** — legacy of "Red Rot" pandemic (−2 120 – −2 105)
- **Ethics of sapience** — research on minds of all three species strictly regulated (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 14])
- **Limited AI** — artificial intelligence created without self-awareness, within the Law of Accord

Not a prohibition of knowledge but an institutional framework developed from historical experience.
