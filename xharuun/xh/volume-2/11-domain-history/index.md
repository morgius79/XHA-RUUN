## 11.2 History of Formation

Boundaries reflect historical-cultural differences formed over millennia:

- **Kharak-Thar** — oldest domain, core of First Empire, site of Khar-Vexar's founding (−7 800); the "heart"
- **Kharak-Tor** — agrarian domain, separated from central Kharak (+120)
- **Rhuval-Ven** — trade center of Rhuval, heir of Serrin region; joined Unity Treaty (−750)
- **Rhuval-Irkhen** — industrial domain around the city of Irkhen (+200)
- **Vexar-Shan** — technological domain, center of Engineers and Artists guilds; joined Unity last (+850)
- **Vexar-Mor** — energy domain of eastern Vexar (+1 200)
- **Island** — Khal-Tor archipelago, oldest cultural center (settled since −5 000)
- **Polar** — scientific stations of polar regions (+2 500), without traditional permanent population

## 11.4 Domains and Guilds

Political system balances two cross-sections: **domains** (territorial) and **guilds** (professional). Each guild has headquarters in a specific domain (Miners Guild in Rhuval-Irkhen, Artists Guild in Vexar-Shan), linking economic and territorial structures (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 08, "Xa-Vokh The-El"]).
