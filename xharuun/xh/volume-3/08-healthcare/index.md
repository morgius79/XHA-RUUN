## 8.3 Prevention and Public Health

Post-scarcity society emphasizes prevention:

- **Sanitary norms** — the historical legacy of epidemiological experience
- **Public nutrition** — food quality control
- **Screening** — early detection of hereditary risks
- **Mental health** — support through the guilds

---

## 8.4 Ethics and Limits

Within ethical frameworks:

- **Right of refusal** — no intervention is forced
- **Three species** — protocols account for all species' biology
- **Rejuvenation** — available but voluntary; a public discussion of its social consequences continues
