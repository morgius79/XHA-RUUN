## 5.2 Functions of the Domains

| Function | Center | Regions |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Legislation | Council Charter | Local norms |
| Taxes | Guilds | 30% — region |
| Education | Scholars Guild | Local schools |
| Healthcare | Medic Guild | Hospitals |
| Infrastructure | Builders Guild | Road network |

Distribution of functions = a model of federalism: the Council and guilds set common standards; the domains implement them locally with regard to local conditions. Tax system directs **30%** of collected funds to regional budgets.

---

## 5.3 Domain Autonomy

Autonomy covers:

- **Culture** — languages, traditions, festivals
- **Education** — local schools and universities
- **Urban planning** — city layouts
- **Local law** — norms not contradicting the canon

The limit of autonomy — unity of measures, defense, foreign policy, and basic legislation.

---

## 5.4 Domains and Representation

Each domain delegates representatives to the **Region Chamber** (→ rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 02, "Rass-Vokh-Sha"]). Distribution by population: larger domains have more representatives, but no domain can block a decision alone.

---

## 5.5 Economic Specialization

- **Kharak-Thar** — administrative and scientific center
- **Kharak-Tor** — agriculture
- **Rhuval-Ven** — trade
- **Rhuval-Irkhen** — industry
- **Vexar-Shan** — technology
- **Vexar-Mor** — energy
- **Island** — culture and seafaring
- **Polar** — science and resources

Specialization ensures complementarity — the economic necessity of unity.
