## 2.1 Composition

| Role | Count | Elected by | Term |
|------|-------|-----------|------|
| Council Chair | 1 | The Council | 10 years |
| Guild Chamber | 24 (2 from each of 12 guilds) | The guilds | 8 years |
| Region Chamber | 25 (from eight domains) | The domains | 6 years |
| Keeper of Knowledge | 1 | Lifelong (appointed) | Until departure |

Total **49 members** (24 + 25) plus the Keeper without vote. The 24/25 distribution is a deliberate bias toward the regions: guilds = professional cross-section, domains = territorial; neither side can dominate without the other's consent.

---

## 2.2 Decision-Making Process

1. **Submitting a question** — any member of the Council
2. **Discussion** — minimum 3 days (constitutional questions — 30 days)
3. **Voting** — simple majority in each chamber
4. **Keeper's veto** — if a decision contradicts the canon, the Keeper suspends it and returns it for reconsideration
5. **Publication** — per the Law of Knowledge

Key decisions (Charter amendments, interspecies questions, planetary programs) require consent of **both chambers** and a qualified majority (**2/3**).

---

## 2.3 The Guild Chamber

**24 members, 2 from each of the 12 guilds.** Delegates chosen by internal procedures; must represent not narrow guild interests but the professional cross-section of society as a whole.

Chamber's purview:
- Economy, science, technology
- Quality standards and professional norms
- Education and healthcare
- Space programs

---

## 2.4 The Region Chamber

**25 members** from eight domains (distribution by population; larger domains have more representatives).

Chamber's purview:
- Territorial structure
- Cultural autonomy of domains
- Demographic policy
- Center-periphery balance

---

## 2.5 The Keeper of Knowledge

A lifelong constitutional office (→ rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 01, "Rass-Vokh Khal-sha"]). Without vote, but with the right of veto on canon-conformity questions.

---

## 2.6 Committees and Commissions

Standing and ad-hoc committees for decision preparation:

- **Planning Committee** — long-term programs
- **Ethics Committee** — science and interspecies questions
- **Canon Commission** — jointly with the Keeper
- **Budget Committee** — allocation of planetary resources

Committees gather expertise from guilds and domains and prepare draft decisions for plenary sessions.
