Structure: Council of Unity (49), Executive Committee (12), 8 domains → provinces → districts, 12 guilds, Keeper of Knowledge.

## 1.1 Principles of Governance

| Principle | Description | Implementation |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| Meritocracy | Power by ability | Guild system |
| Collegiality | Decisions made collectively | Council of Unity (49 members) |
| Transparency | Access to information | Law of Knowledge |
| Sustainability | Long-term planning | Growth Commandment |

**Meritocracy** — power based on proven ability, not origin. Guild system (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 08, "Xa-Vokh The-El"]) = institutional foundation: the path to power lies through professional recognition.

**Collegiality** — no body decides unilaterally. Council, domains, guilds make all decisions collectively; key ones require consent of both chambers.

**Transparency** — the Law of Knowledge guarantees access to information (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 12, "Khal-Sha The-El"]). Council decisions published, rationale available to all.

**Sustainability** — long-term planning governed by the Growth Commandment and ecological limits.

## 1.2 Structure of Power

```
        Council of Unity (49)
  24 Guilds + 25 Regions + Keeper (no vote)
                     │
        Executive Committee (12)
                     │
   8 Domains → Provinces → Districts
                     │
         12 Guilds (sectors)
```

**The Council of Unity (Rass-Vokh-Sha)** — the supreme body of **49 members**: 24 delegates from 12 guilds (2 each) + 25 delegates from eight domains. **Keeper of Knowledge** participates without vote — constitutional function: control of decisions' conformity to the canon.

Council Chair elected from among the members for **10 years**, chairs sessions without a casting vote.

---

## 1.3 Executive Power

**Executive Committee (12 members)** — the Council's working body, ensuring execution of its decisions. Formed proportionally: **6 members from guilds, 6 from domains**.

Functions:
- Preparation of decisions and analytical materials
- Coordination of domains' and guilds' activities
- Management of planetary programs (infrastructure, space)
- Interaction with the Keeper of Knowledge

No legislative power — accountable to the Council, can be re-elected.

---

## 1.4 Territorial Structure

Local implementation ensured by **eight domains** (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 11, "Sha-El Xha-Thar"]): domain → province → district.

Domains have broad autonomy: local laws, culture, education. The Council — domain — province vertical is complemented by the horizontal structure of guilds, whose headquarters are distributed across domains.

---

## 1.5 Guilds as Governance Sectors

The twelve guilds = the second circuit of power, representing the professional cross-section. Each guild manages its sector: economy, science, healthcare, infrastructure, culture.

Guilds not only delegate members to the Council but also implement state functions locally: standards, licensing, training, adjudication (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 08, "Xa-Vokh The-El"]).

---

## 1.6 The Role of the Keeper of Knowledge

**The Keeper of Knowledge** — a unique institution: a lifelong office without political power, but with a constitutional function.

The Keeper:
- Ensures Council decisions conform to the canon
- Heads the Keeper Guild (archives, libraries)
- Has the right to suspend a decision contradicting the canon, referring it for reconsideration
- Nominally not elected — appointed by the previous Keeper with Council confirmation

Embodies the principle of the **inviolability of history**: knowledge of the past is protected from political expediency.
