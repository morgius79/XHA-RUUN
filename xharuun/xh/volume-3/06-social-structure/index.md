# Thexar-III: Ruun-El — Sha-El 06: Xha-Mor The-Rass — Social Structure

> *"There are no castes. There are no estates. There is only your contribution to the common cause — and the respect you have earned."*
> — From the Code of the Council of Unity

---

**Ruun-ak rass:** → rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 01, "Rass-Vokh Khal-sha"], → rhu [Thexar-III, Sha-El 04, "Ruun-El Xha-Khal"]
**Volume:** 25 sha-ruun
**Mor-ven:** Result of a conscious rejection of the estate model. Formally casteless: status determined by contribution, education, professional recognition — not origin. Mechanism = guild system, ensuring high social mobility. Status factors: guild 40%, merit 30%, education 20%, origin 10%.

---

## 6.1 Formal Structure

Formally casteless. Social status determined by:

| Factor | Weight | Comment |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Guild | 40% | Membership and rank |
| Merit | 30% | Contribution to society |
| Education | 20% | Level of knowledge |
| Origin | 10% | Family (minimal influence) |

Guild membership and rank = main status factor (40%). Origin deliberately minimized (10%) — a lesson of history: the estate-based First Empire fell partly because of the elite-people gap (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 04, "Thar-Xa-Khal"]).

---

## 6.2 Social Mobility

High mobility: **60% of guild members** come from families of a different status.

| Path | Difficulty | Time |
|------|-----------|------|
| Education → Guild | Low | 10–15 years |
| Merit → Recognition | Medium | 5–20 years |
| Guild change | Medium | 1–3 years |
| Entrepreneurship | Low | Any |

Key mobility channel — **education**: free and accessible, it allows anyone to enter a guild by ability rather than origin.

---

## 6.3 Family and Kinship

In post-scarcity society the family is not an economic unit — it is based on emotional bonds. The three Xha'Ruun sexes form diverse family structures (→ rhu [Thexar-I, Ruun 08, "Xa-Sha-Ven"]). The state supports families through the guilds but does not control their structure.

---

## 6.4 Equality of Species

Includes all three sapient species (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 14, "Xha-Sha Xha-Mor"]). Xha'ri, Kel'vash, Moryn equal before the law; social status within each species determined by the same factors. Interspecies relations governed by the Law of Accord.

---

## 6.5 Institutional Protection

Equality protected institutionally:

- **The canon** — prohibition of discrimination by origin and species
- **Guild courts** — protection of members' rights
- **The Keeper of Knowledge** — canon conformity control
- **Educational accessibility** — removal of mobility barriers
