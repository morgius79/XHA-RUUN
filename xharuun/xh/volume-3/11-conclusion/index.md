## Key Ideas

1. **Meritocracy** — power by ability, not origin; the guild system is the institutional foundation.
2. **Post-scarcity** — basic resources available to all; the economic problem shifted to meaning.
3. **Balance** — guilds (professional cross-section) and domains (territorial) balanced in the Council.
4. **Sustainability** — ecological ethics (the Law of Silence), demographic regulation (the Growth Commandment).
5. **Knowledge as value** — education, science, and history — the highest priorities.

---

## Society and History

The social model is not an abstract ideal but a response to specific historical lessons: the fall of the First Empire (the limit of inequality and extensive growth), the dark ages (the value of knowledge), fragmentation (the value of unity). Every institution of modern society is the result of processing catastrophic experience (→ rhu [Thexar-II, Sha-El 13, "Mor-El"]).

---

## Looking Ahead

New challenges:

- **Stellar expansion** — will the social model carry itself to other worlds?
- **Interspecies accord** — will the balance of the three species survive?
- **The meaning of abundance** — what does a society do when material needs are satisfied?

The answers will define the next chapter of history.
