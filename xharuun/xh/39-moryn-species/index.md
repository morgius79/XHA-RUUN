## 39.1 Discovery and Status

Moryn are the third sentient species of Théxar, inhabiting the lightless depths of the Gharra ocean. Where light never arrives and water pressure is measured in hundreds of atmospheres, they built a civilization founded not on sound and not on writing, but on the **light of their own bodies**. Their existence is a living refutation of the idea that intelligence requires land, fire, and writing.

| Event | Year (E.U.) |
|-------|-----------|
| First sightings (from fishing vessels, deep-water glow) | ~−3 000 |
| Confirmed contact (Research Guild bathyscaphe) | −1 200 |
| Establishment of permanent contact | −800 |
| Admission to the Council of Unity | +1 500 |

Before confirmed contact, the glow in Gharra's depths was explained for centuries as a natural phenomenon — the "abyssal lights" of Kharak and Rhuval mythology. Only a Research Guild bathyscaphe descent in −1,200 recorded the deliberate, observer-responsive character of the glow.

## 39.2 Habitat

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Region | Deep zone of the Gharra ocean (Southern) |
| Depth | 1,200–3,000 sha |
| Pressure | 120–300 atm |
| Temperature | 2–8°X |
| Illumination | 0 (aphotic zone) |
| Salinity | Elevated (bottom brine pools) |

Moryn avoid shelf and surface waters — their physiology is built for constant high pressure, and an ascent to the surface is lethal for an adult (decompression injury). Major settlements cluster around hydrothermal vents on the Gharra seafloor, which provide heat and chemical energy for the chemoautotrophic symbionts Moryn feed on (→ rhu [Ruun 14, "Xa-Vek Ak Théxar"]).

## 39.3 Biochemistry and Physiology

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Cell membrane | Silicon-organic (withstands pressure of 300+ atm) |
| Metabolism | 3× slower than Xha'Ruun |
| Optimal temperature | 2–8°X |
| Energy source | Chemoautotrophic symbiotic bacteria + organic filtration |
| Pigmentation | None (no function in total absence of light) |

**Pressure resistance:** the silicon-organic membrane is functionally analogous to the Kel'vash solution (→ rhu [Ruun 38, "Kel'vash Sha-Mor"]), but serves an opposite purpose: not protection against desiccation, but preservation of cellular integrity under enormous hydrostatic pressure. Moryn intracellular fluids are isosmotic with the environment — the organism does not "resist" pressure but balances it from within.

**Slow metabolism and time:** metabolism runs 3× slower than Xha'Ruun — an adaptation to a cold, energy-poor environment. What looks to a Xha'Ruun observer like an unhurried "dance" is, to the Moryn themselves, an ordinary pace of everyday conversation.

## 39.4 Bioluminescent Communication

**Key fact:** Moryn have no distinction between "speech" and "writing." Their language is a sequence of color patterns generated by chromatophore organs across the body's surface, synchronized with movement. Xha'Ruun call it a "dance," but for the Moryn it is the only form of thought observable from outside.

| Language component | Mechanism | Xha'Ruun analogue |
|---------------------|-----------|---------------------|
| "Phonemes" | Discrete color flashes (7 base hues) | Sound phonemes |
| "Syntax" | Sequence and speed of color change | Word order |
| "Intonation" | Amplitude and area of the glow | Tone of voice |
| "Writing" | Long-duration bioluminescent marks on rock surfaces (used by some colonies) | Writing |

**Bioluminescent organs:** specialized chromatophore organs, densest on the dorsal side and around the sensory ridges. The emission spectrum ranges from deep blue (450 nm) to dark red (700 nm), and Moryn can distinguish hues inaccessible even to Xha'Ruun tetrachromatic vision with its UV channel (→ rhu [Ruun 05, "Xha'Ruun Sha-Mor"]) — their photoreceptors are adapted to extremely low light and can detect single photons.

## 39.5 Electroreception and Navigation

In total darkness, electroreception supplements bioluminescent vision as the primary channel of orientation:

| Application | Range | Precision |
|-------------|-------|-----------|
| Detecting another individual's "heartbeat" | up to 40 sha | High |
| Mapping seafloor terrain | up to 100 sha | Medium |
| Detecting hydrothermal vents (via electrochemical gradient) | up to 300 sha | High |

Moryn electroreceptor organs are an order of magnitude more sensitive than the rudimentary Xha'Ruun analogue — an evolutionary necessity where neither sound nor sunlight was ever available.

## 39.6 Society and Culture

Because of bioluminescent communication, Moryn lack a concept of "private," non-public thought — any glow is visible to everyone present simultaneously. This produced a strongly collectivist culture, perceived by Xha'Ruun observers as almost telepathic:

- Decisions are made through synchronous "consonance" of color patterns across the whole group.
- Individual privacy is expressed spatially (solitude = physical distance) rather than informationally.
- Moryn art and religion are built on multilayered light compositions — "underwater symphonies of color."

**Lifespan:** estimated at 300–400 years — intermediate between Xha'Ruun (150–200) and Kel'vash (800–1,200), consistent with their intermediate metabolic rate.

## 39.7 Relations with Xha'Ruun and Kel'vash

Three main barriers:

1. **Language barrier** — translation between sound and bioluminescence requires specialized transluminator devices at the Council's deep-sea stations.
2. **Temporal barrier** — Moryn time perception (metabolism 3× slower) requires slowing dialogue roughly threefold.
3. **Ethical barrier** — the collectivist, "transparent" ethic (where a hidden thought is physically impossible) sits uneasily with Xha'Ruun notions of personal autonomy and privacy.

Despite the barriers, the Moryn have been a full member of the Council of Unity since +1 500, and no decision concerning Théxar's oceans is made without their participation.

## 39.8 Ecological Role

Moryn occupy the deep-sea chemosynthetic niche — unavailable to photosynthetic life and extremely hostile to land-based forms. Their symbiosis with chemoautotrophic bacteria of hydrothermal vents makes them a key link in Gharra's benthic ecosystems, analogous to the Xha'Ruun role on land (→ rhu [Ruun 21, "Xa-The Xa-Vokh Mor-Ven"]).
