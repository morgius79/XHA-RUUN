## 3.2 Crystal Memory (Ven-Khal)

Information is stored in the **lattice defects** of grown silicon-carbide crystals.

| Parameter | Ven-Khal crystal |
|-----------|------------------|
| Capacity | 10¹⁸ TB (1 cm³) |
| Durability | >10⁹ years (at room T) |
| Read energy | 0 (optical reading) |
| Record destruction | Only via nuclear decay |

Read by passing H₂S laser light through the crystal; defects distort the light, information is reconstructed. A single 1 cm³ crystal holds all knowledge ever produced by humanity — repeated 10 000 times.

## 3.3 Emotional Transmission (Sha-Ven)

A technology that transmits not just information but **sensory-emotional experience** — total immersion into the consciousness of another being. Applications: education (living through a great engineer's experience), diplomacy, art, memory (preserving the personality of the deceased — but not restoring it, a philosophical prohibition).

## 3.4 Moryn Language — Without Technology

Moryn communicate through **bioluminescent patterns** visible to depths of 3 000 m; a single flash can convey information equivalent to 10 000 words. Translation uses **living translators** — modified organisms symbiotizing with both species.
