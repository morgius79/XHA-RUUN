**Mor-ven:** Strong AI (AGI) is forbidden — not for technological but **ethical** reasons: consciousness created in a machine has no evolutionary history, is not connected to the biosphere, and cannot possess empathy. Instead, the Xha'Ruun took a different path: grown collective intelligence.

---

## 5.1 Philosophical Prohibition

Artificial consciousness is forbidden because it has no evolutionary history, is disconnected from the biosphere, and cannot possess empathy.

---

## 5.2 Xha-Rass — Grown Collective Intelligence

A symbiotic network of bio-colonies, crystal memory, and living neural cultures grown from Xha'ri cells:

- **240** bio-colonies (computing nodes)
- **48** crystal memory banks
- Neural cultures from **12** volunteer donors

Xha-Rass **does not possess self-awareness**, but can sense the biosphere, predict natural phenomena with >99% accuracy, answer complex questions, and generate new hypotheses.

**What Xha-Rass cannot do:** have desires, make ethical decisions, reproduce, escape control (physiologically constrained).

---

## 5.3 Restriction as Philosophy

The civilization consciously limits technologies that could disrupt the natural order (CANON.md §4.4):

| Prohibition | Reason |
|-------------|--------|
| Strong AI | Cannot possess empathy |
| Personality cloning | Violates individual integrity |
| Memory overwriting | Violates identity |
| Immortality | Violates evolutionary cycles |
| FTL | Violates causality |
