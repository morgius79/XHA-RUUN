# Thexar-VI: Khal-Vex-El — Sha-El 09: Vex-Thel Khal-sha — Construction

> *"We do not erect buildings. We plant them. A house is a tree whose roots reach into the foundation and whose crown reaches into the future. The builder is a gardener, not a stonecutter."*
> — Guild of Builders, "Gardeners of Stone," +2 600 Y.E.

**Mor-ven:** Construction is the oldest engineering discipline — settlements appear 11 500 years before the present. Over twelve millennia the method changed, not the scale: the modern builder does not lay stone upon stone but **grows** the structure from living material. The change looks cosmetic, but it changed everything — design, service life, profession, aesthetics.

## 9.1 From Masonry to Growing

| Era | Method | Material | Duration | Characteristic Form |
|-----|--------|----------|----------|---------------------|
| Ancient (−12 000 – −9 200) | Masonry | Stone, bone | Years | Megaliths |
| Imperial (−6 200 – −2 150) | Masonry, arch | Basalt, marble | Years | Colonnades |
| Industrial (−1 200 – +850) | Assembly, casting | Steel, concrete | Months | Frames, towers |
| Transitional (+850 – +1 812) | Assembly + planting | Composites, titanium | Months | Hybrid forms |
| Modern (+1 812 – present) | Growing | Thar-Vex, living composites | Days-months | Organic structures |

The turning point **+1 812** — the first grown load-bearing beam. From it a house is "planted."

## 9.2 The Stages of Construction

| Stage | Duration | Content |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Design | 1–6 years | Form, purpose, a "garden plan" |
| Planting | Days | Foundation "seed" laid in the soil |
| Growth | 6–108 days | Structure grows from a scaffold form |
| Maturation | 1–2 years | The material "tunes in," strength peaks |
| Occupation | — | Only after the honesty test |

The **foundation** is not laid but planted; the structure grows from a temporary scaffold ("form") that determines the shape. Growth time by the grade of Thar-Vex: household 6 days, residential 36, load-bearing 108, bridges 216 (→ rhu [Thexar-VI, Sha-El 07, "Khal-Thel Sha-Thar"]).

## 9.4 The Building Keeper

Every large structure is cared for by a **keeper** — a professional who communicates with the building, reads its "complaints," and prescribes "feeding" (silicon solution, H₂S traces). The keeper's qualification: the ability to perceive the building's state through its "voice" — resonance, color, smell. The keeper is a gardener, not an engineer; their skills border on art.

## 9.5 Urban Planning

| Principle | Content |
|-----------|---------|
| Radial-hexagonal | Cities as hexagons in a honeycomb |
| The "green spine" | A park avenue through the city |
| Living quarters | Surround the center, not divide it |
| Height gradient | Center → periphery, no walls |
| Sound design | Acoustics built into the plan |

**Radial-hexagonal planning** — streets radiate from the center, split into hexagons. The **"green spine"** — a continuous park avenue dividing the city in half, a legacy of the "garden city" movement of +2 100.

## 9.6 Limits of Construction

| Limit | Reason |
|-------|--------|
| Below −60 °C | Thar-Vex fails |
| Above 40 Gy/year | Radiation degrades |
| Height by resonance | Above ~40 km the material "sings" |
| Contact with melts | Destruction |
| No acceleration | Doubling the rate reduces strength 4× |

The theoretical **height limit** of a grown structure — limited by gravitational resonance (~40 km); the practical maximum is the orbital elevator, grown in a state of gravitational equilibrium (→ rhu [Thexar-VI, Sha-El 02, "Thar-Vex Khal-sha"]).
