## 1.1 Fundamental Difference

Three pillars, each alien to terrestrial thinking:

1. **Symbiogenesis** — technologies are **grown**. Any artifact is a modified organism or symbiotic colony. A house is a living being programmed to grow into the right shape; a ship is a symbiont born for flight.
2. **Resonance Physics** — technology operates with **resonances of fundamental fields**. Gravity, magnetism, nuclear forces — different frequencies of a single field.
3. **Information as Matter** — information is a physical entity. To record information is to create matter; to erase it is to destroy.
