**Mor-ven:** Xha'Ruun do not build spaceships — they **grow** them. Xha-Vokh ("Spirit-People") are modified organisms adapted for the vacuum: silicon-organic exoskeleton, photosynthetic membrane, gas bladder. Space expansion is symbiotic, a "closed loop" from birth to return.

**Life cycle:** embryos grow for 12 years in orbital "shipyards" (nutrient pools); a young Xha-Vokh (~2 km wingspan) serves short voyages; after 200 years it becomes interplanetary. On death, the body burns up over Théxar — the silicon-organic ash falls to the surface, becoming soil: a closed loop.

---

## 6.2 Orbital Forest

At altitudes of 350–1 200 km grows the **Orbital Forest**: 24 living symbiotic stations, Xha-Vokh on "pasture," crystal reflectors, GR-resonators. Stations use **bio-membranes** letting air pass one way; pressure is maintained by the organism's metabolism.

---

## 6.3 Serrin-Ur and Thel

**Serrin** (moon, 425 km): no bases — a colony of Xha-Vokh attached to the surface, a "nursery" where young spend the first 50 years feeding on regolith.

**Thel** (moon, 178 km): a source of He-3; extraction by symbiotic organisms that filter regolith. Not a single building — only living structures.

---

## 6.4 Interstellar Plans

As of +4 028, no interstellar flights (light speed is absolute, FTL impossible). The **"Seeding"** project: sending Xha-Vokh embryos to nearby stars in suspended animation, with a "wake" program upon finding a suitable planet.
