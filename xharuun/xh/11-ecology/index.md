## 11.6 Unique Ecosystem

~10% surface area.

| Shan | Ven |
|------|-----|
| Biomass | ~2 545.5 ru/mo² |
| Canopy | 73–98 sha |
| Layers | **6** |

**6-layer structure:**
1. Emergent (>98 sha) — Six-winged Oak
2. Upper canopy (49–98 sha) — main crown
3. Middle canopy (18–49 sha) — liana, epiphyte
4. Lower canopy (6–18 sha) — shade-tolerant
5. Understory (1.2–6.1 sha) — shrub
6. Forest floor (0–1.2 sha) — moss, fern, bacteria

### 11.6.2 Cave Ecosystem

Kharak + Vexar karst, ~9 914 mo² total. Some caves isolated M cycle.

| Character | Adaptation |
|-----------|-----------|
| Light | Absent → blind, depigmented |
| Energy | H₂S chemosynth (90%), guano (10%) |
| Species size | Smaller than terrestrial |
| Endemism | ~95% cave endemic |
| Lifespan | 3–5× terrestrial |

Species: Blind Six-legged Crab (Xhel-kreb, 17 pa), Cave Filter Worm (Xhel-thal, 0.61–3.66 sha), Bioluminescent Moss (Xhel-khar, faint blue glow).

### 11.6.3 Hydrothermal Oases

Gharra ocean, 0.71–1.52 mo depth, ~6 vent field. Black smoker T ≤252°X, pH 2.5. Ecosystem completely photosynthesis-independent. H₂S, Fe, Mn, Si emission → Si-tube worm cluster (2.4 sha).
