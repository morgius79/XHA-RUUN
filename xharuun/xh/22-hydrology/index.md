## 22.1 River Systems

### 22.1.1 Largest Rivers (12 major)

Kharak continent carries ~24% basin via Khar-Tor.

### 22.1.2 Khar-Tor River — Largest on Planet

| Shan | Ven |
|------|-----|
| Source | Khar-Tor glaciers, 7 560 sha |
| Mouth | Serrin Sea |
| Basin | ~24% Kharak |
| Length | 870 mo |

**Regime:**
- Recharge: glacial 45% + rain 40% + groundwater 15%
- High water (Tor-Ven): 69 000 sha³/s
- Low water (Mor-Khal): 11 300 sha³/s
- Solid runoff: ~194 bln kh/y (volcanic ash)

**Economy:**
- Irrigation 49 600 mo²
- HEP cascade (6 stations, 48 power units)
- Navigable 427 mo from mouth

### 22.1.3 Thar River — Sacred River

First proto-Kharite settlements (→ EV-0001). Capital Khar-Vexar in delta.
