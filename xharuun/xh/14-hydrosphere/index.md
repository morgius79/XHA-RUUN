### 14.2.1 Thash Trench

**Deepest point on Théxar: 12 010 sha.** Rhuval/Gharra plate subduction. Pressure ~952 tyazh, T 46.6°X (1.6°X above analog trench, geothermal-mor). Unique chemosynthesis ecosystem.

### 14.2.2 Circulation

- **Gharra Circumpolar Current** — powerful ring around Vexar S, partial isolation
- **Ven-Khal Current** — cold deep, connects Thalas ↔ Gharra
- **Deep upwelling** W Vexar → nutrient rise

## 14.3 Seas

**5 Seas:**

| Sea | Area (mo²) | Avg depth (sha) | Ocean | Sha |
|-----|-----------|-----------------|-------|-----|
| **Serrin** | 132 200 | 1 463 | Thalas S | Warm, shallow, S Kharak/N Rhuval |
| **Irkhen** | 115 700 | 2 195 | Thalas E | Stormy, archipelago, cultural crossroad |
| **Thash** | 78 500 | 2 927 | Gharra W | Deep, contains Thash Trench |
| **Ven-Khal** | 62 000 | 1 098 | Gharra → Thalas | Strait sea, 2-ocean connect |
| **Koss** | 40 500 | 732 | Gharra S | Cold, polar, krill-rich |

**Serrin Sea** — warmest, most productive (≈320 g C/m²/y). Kharak river inflow (Thar), well-developed shelf, stable upwelling both coast, T 57.0–59.2°X. Named for planet's largest satellite.

**Irkhen Sea** — strong tidal current (≤67 sha/mig in strait), >60 d/y storm with wave >3.7 sha. Key trade route Kharak/Rhuval.

**Ven-Khal Sea** — only 2-ocean connect, strait 85 mo narrowest.

> **Rass-Ven:** ~8% total inter-ocean water exchange passes through Ven-Khal Strait. Critical for thermohaline circulation.

## 14.4 Currents ven Thermohaline Circulation

### 14.4.1 Global Conveyor

**Stages:**
1. Warm surface Thalas → N (Tor Current), heat release to atm
2. Cold, dense water sinks N Thalas latitude (deep convection)
3. Deep current → S, passes Ven-Khal → Gharra
4. Gharra partial upwelling → cycle close

**Parameters:**

| Shan | Ven |
|------|-----|
| Deep convection zone | Thalas 30°–50°N |
| Avg speed (deep) | 10.2 pa/mig |
| Full cycle | **~2 500 y** |
| Heat transport | ~0.8 PW |
| Deep T | 46.6°X |

> **Rass-Ven:** Théxar cycle 2.5× slower (weaker wind forcing, smaller salinity gradient).
