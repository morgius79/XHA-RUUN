## 14.2 Key Ideas

1. **One code, one biosphere.** All Théxaran life shares the 6-nucleotide genetic code; symbiogenesis is the rule, not the exception.
2. **Three sapient species, one ecology.** Xha'Ruun (carbon), Kel'vash (silicon-organic), Moryn (biophotonic) all descend from the same Thermochromadae radiation.
3. **Extremophiles are the mainstream.** Subglacial, cave, and deep-marine ecosystems host as many species as the surface, all rooted in chemosynthesis.
4. **Five mass extinctions, one lesson.** Every extinction reshaped but did not erase the Thermochromadae body plan.
5. **The biosphere as a body.** C-N-S-Si cycles keep Théxar in a homeostatic state older than the First Empire.
