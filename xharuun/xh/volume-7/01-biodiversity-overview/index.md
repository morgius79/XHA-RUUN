## 01.2 Unique Features of the Biosphere

### 01.2.1 Six-Letter Genetic Code

Théxar's biosphere uses 6 nucleotides (G, C, A, T, P, X) instead of 4.

**Consequences:**
- 216 codons (instead of 64)
- 24 proteinogenic amino acids (instead of 20)
- 5× higher genome noise immunity
- Protein complexity 30–40% higher

### 01.2.2 Sulfur Cycle

H₂S is not a toxin but a fully functional metabolic substrate; H₂S chemosynthesis is as widespread as photosynthesis.

### 01.2.3 D-Chirality

Amino acids are D-forms, sugars are L-forms. Opposite chirality means Earth organisms cannot interact with Théxar's biosphere at the molecular level.
