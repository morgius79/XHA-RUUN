## Ruun Ven

1. The Xha'Ruun body plan is hexapodal, with three specialized limb pairs — lower for locomotion, middle for manipulation, upper as wing-manipulators.
2. Core anatomy includes two hearts and four lungs, reflecting redundancy shaped by millions of evolutionary cycles.
3. Reproductive biology is trisexual: male (♂), female (♀), and neuter (⚲), each with distinct roles.
4. The neuter sex forms the majority (65%) of the population and underpins Xha'Ruun social structure through care and labor.
5. Digit counts differ by limb pair (3-4-3), with upper limbs bearing claws for aerial and precise use.
6. Anatomical redundancy and role-specialized biology are viewed by the Academy as evolutionary answers to environmental challenge, not excess.
