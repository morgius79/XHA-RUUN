### 19.2.1 Balance Structure

**Thorium cycle (Th → U-233):**
- Reserve: 10.9 M ru (52% Kharak)
- Tech: molten salt reactor (MSR)
- Burnup: ~98%
- Waste: 1/100 alt cycle
- Cost: **0.8 ¢/kWh**

- Reserve: ~60.6 k ru (Thel regolith)
